# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run from what happened. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

- Evolutions are the power spike: a weapon at level 8 plus its catalyst, then the next chest, opened while the bar is full, moving, and off the densest tiles. Open it on the same beat the pair is ready. A chest carried into the next boss stays closed.
- On this Daily the chain is Overgrowth, then Jet Fryer, then Big Fry. Fertilizer once, Seeker Sprouts to 8, then one chest. Overgrowth around 4:00 passed Late Blight on the long runs. This run died at 3:16 with sprouts at 5/8 because Starch Plating took four levels. A second Starch Plating before Overgrowth is a leak; reroll instead. Open the chest only once Seeker Sprouts is 8: before Colorado Queen (~3:00) when the pair is ready, otherwise before Late Blight (~6:00).
- Until a consult sees Fertilizer owned, Seeker Sprouts stays out of the sentence and off want. Want is Fertilizer, Gravy Heart, Hearty Helping, and Hash Rush. Copy button text exactly. The clause is: take Fertilizer only if it is not already owned; if it is already owned, do not take it again; otherwise take Gravy Heart, then Hearty Helping or Hash Rush; otherwise reroll. Never write "take X over Gravy Heart" and never write "refuse" without reroll. Fertilizer stayed 1/5 under that clause. Naming sprouts at 0:30 on an earlier run got Fertilizer skipped.
- Once Fertilizer is owned, Fertilizer leaves the sentence. Want is Seeker Sprouts, Gravy Heart, Hearty Helping, and Hash Rush, and Starch Plating stays off want until Overgrowth exists. While sprouts are below 8, take Seeker Sprouts when offered; when it is absent take Gravy Heart; when both are absent take Hearty Helping or Hash Rush only if it is not already owned; otherwise reroll. Sprouts sat at 5/8 from 2:45 while Starch Plating went to 4/5, and the chest stayed closed.
- After Overgrowth, take Hot Start once if it is missing, then name only Afterburner Fries until it is 8, then one chest for Jet Fryer. A second level into Hot Start is a leak. If Hot Start is still unowned by 5:00 and a weapon slot is open, name Small Fry until 8, then one chest for Big Fry. After Overgrowth, Starch Plating joins want behind Gravy Heart, and Hearty Helping and Hash Rush may be stacked.
- Gravy Heart outranks the other sustain cards and may be stacked the whole run whenever a passive slot is open, including when the weapon card is absent. Gravy Heart at 5/5 raised max HP from about 111 to 183 on the run that reached 17:20. This run ended near 111 HP with Starch Plating 4/5 and no Gravy Heart; fry-flies and the queen were larger than that bar.
- Six weapons and six passives. After six passives exist, a missing catalyst can never be taken. An open slot goes to the next planned catalyst if it is still unowned, then to Gravy Heart, Hearty Helping, Hash Rush, and, after Overgrowth, Starch Plating. Gravy Splash is never a take. This run kept it out.
- Hold Tot Toss, Tot Stampede, Chives, Fry Knives, Reset Button, Gravy Splash, Butter Pat, Couch Potato, Frozen Fries, Tailgate Fries, and Slow Cooker. A leak at 8 makes the nearest chest the wrong evolution. Leave that chest closed while the planned weapon can still reach 8.
- If the planned catalyst is absent and one leaked pair is already 8 with its catalyst maxed, open that one chest while the bar is full, health is steady, and the near ring is empty, then walk past every later chest. Stop after that chest.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still while any enemy is inside 60. A heading that is still taking hits is a standstill. This death was Colorado Queen closing from 65 to 14 while fry-flies hit: health went from 74% to 7% in 30 seconds, with about 30 enemies within 60.
- The first clause names one destination, in this order: off a tile where a boss attack will land, then out of hot oil or puddles until the ticks stop, then directly away from a boss inside 80 until it is outside 80, then out of the pack toward food when enemies are inside 60 and health is under two thirds or just fell fast, then sideways off a boss lane, and only then away from the densest side. Empty direction bins are not a dense side and are not a stop.
- While a boss is inside 80, away from that boss is the only destination. Do not add a second walk toward food or off the flies. Fleeing the flies walked into the queen; she did 23 at about 15 units, and the flies did 46.
- If no food is visible, no boss is inside 80, and health is under half, turn away from the side that just dealt damage and keep walking until the hits stop.
- While oil is ticking, the only destination is off the oil, until the ticks stop. Name no food, no boss chase, and no kept heading. A second destination walks back onto the oil. Hot oil did 1 this run after 47, 15, and 43 on the runs before.
- A telegraph under 1.5 seconds overrides the coach line, food, and the crowd. Leave any telegraph first. Never name a one-shot step that is still the plan after the circle is gone.
- A clear near ring is not safety. A fast drop (about half the bar within 30 seconds, or most of the bar within 10 seconds) leaves only telegraph, then oil, then away from a boss inside 80, otherwise away from whatever is dealing the damage. That line names no chest and no shrine. Food does not outheal fry-flies on a 111 bar.
- A chest and a health fallback in one line send the bot to the chest. The coach is read for a while. Write a line that is still correct a minute later: one movement destination first, then a level-up clause that stays correct after the named card is picked. When health is falling, a boss is inside 80, or oil is ticking, the line has no chest.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss attacks are aimed at the tile. Step sideways out of the lane. Distance from the body does not dodge the circle.
- A boss inside 80 is a walk directly away until it is outside 80. Inside 50 is already melee: the queen did 23 from about 15 units. The circle is still the threat when the boss is well outside 80.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a low bar. Step out before food or a chest, and do not turn back onto the oil.
- On this Daily: Amusing around 2:00, Colorado Queen (beetle queen) around 3:00, The Masher around 4:00, Late Blight around 6:00, Sir Strawman around 7:00, The Fryer King around 8:00 with oil, Great Wireworm around 10:00, The Mandoline around 12:00, Deep Freezer around 14:00, Grater Golem around 16:00, Compost Titan around 17:00. Late Blight is passed once Overgrowth exists. On the longer runs the late chip was Great Wireworm, harvest mice, field rats, and fry-flies. Deep Freezer and Compost Titan are the late circles; mud golems and grease fiends finish a low bar.
- Circles first, then out of oil, then away from a boss inside 80, then out of the pack. Falling health outranks a chest, a shrine, and a gap in the crowd.
