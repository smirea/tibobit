# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run from what happened. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

- Evolutions are the power spike: a weapon at level 8 plus its catalyst, then the next chest, opened while the bar is full, moving, and off the densest tiles. Open it on the same beat the pair is ready. A chest carried into the next boss stays closed.
- On this Daily the chain is Overgrowth, then Jet Fryer, then Big Fry. Fertilizer once, Seeker Sprouts to 8, then one chest before Late Blight (~6:00). Overgrowth around 4:00 passed Late Blight on the long runs. This run reached Seeker Sprouts 8 at 7:02 on a 22% bar, so the chest stayed closed and Late Blight was not passed.
- A name in want is taken every time it is offered until the next consult. The once-only sentence does not beat that mark: Fertilizer in want from 0:30 went to 4/5 by 2:00 and 5/5 by 3:04. Naming Seeker Sprouts before Fertilizer is owned skips Fertilizer. Until a consult shows Fertilizer owned, leave want empty. Copy button text exactly. The clause has to stay true across the gap to the next consult: if Fertilizer is not owned, take Fertilizer; if Fertilizer is owned and Seeker Sprouts is below 8, take Seeker Sprouts; when Seeker Sprouts is absent, take Gravy Heart; otherwise reroll. Never write a line that takes another card over Gravy Heart, and never write refuse without reroll.
- Once a consult shows Fertilizer owned, Fertilizer leaves the sentence and stays off want. While sprouts are below 8, want is Seeker Sprouts and Gravy Heart. Take Seeker Sprouts when offered; when it is absent take Gravy Heart; otherwise reroll. Hearty Helping, Hash Rush, Hot Start, and Starch Plating stay off want until Gravy Heart is owned and Overgrowth exists.
- All six passive slots were full by 5:00: Fertilizer 5/5, Hot Start 3/5, Hearty Helping 2/5, Butter Pat 3/5, GPU 1/5, Packed Pan 1/5. Gravy Heart was wanted for most of the run and still ended unowned, so the bar stayed about 111. Gravy Heart at 5/5 raised max HP from about 111 to 183 on the run that reached 17:20. Sir Strawman did about 27 in three seconds on a 12/111 bar. After six passives exist, a missing Gravy Heart can never be taken.
- Seeker Sprouts sat at 7/8 from 5:00 until 7:02 while Locked Gatling reached 7/8 and Grab Bag 2/8. Those names were not in want, and otherwise reroll did not stop them. That missing sprout level is the Late Blight window. If the offer is not the wanted name, reroll. Leave every chest closed until sprouts are 8. If sprouts are already 8 and the bar is falling, reroll every offer and leave the chest closed.
- After Overgrowth, take Hot Start once if it is missing, then name only Afterburner Fries until it is 8, then one chest for Jet Fryer. A second Hot Start is a leak. Any Hot Start before Overgrowth is a leaked slot. If Hot Start is still unowned by 5:00 and a weapon slot is open, name Small Fry until 8, then one chest for Big Fry. After Overgrowth and after Gravy Heart is owned, stack Gravy Heart, then Starch Plating, Hearty Helping, and Hash Rush.
- Gravy Heart outranks the other sustain cards and may be stacked the whole run whenever a passive slot is open, including when the weapon card is absent.
- Six weapons and six passives. An open slot goes to Gravy Heart until it is owned, then to the next planned catalyst, then Hearty Helping, Hash Rush, and, after Overgrowth, Starch Plating. Gravy Splash is never a take.
- Hold Tot Toss, Tot Stampede, Chives, Fry Knives, Reset Button, Gravy Splash, Butter Pat, Couch Potato, Frozen Fries, Tailgate Fries, Slow Cooker, Locked Gatling, Grab Bag, GPU, and Packed Pan. A leak at 8 makes the nearest chest the wrong evolution. Leave that chest closed while the planned weapon can still reach 8.
- If the planned catalyst is absent and one leaked pair is already 8 with its catalyst maxed, open that one chest while the bar is full, health is steady, and the near ring is empty, then walk past every later chest. Stop after that chest.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still while any enemy is inside 60. A heading that is still taking hits is a standstill.
- The line names one destination, then one level-up clause that stays correct after the named card is picked. A menu of telegraph, oil, boss, and crowd is four plans. From 5:00 the line carried all four, and hot oil still did 37. The run where off the oil was the only destination took 1 from oil.
- Destination order, and only the first one that applies: off a tile where a boss attack will land; out of hot oil until the ticks stop; directly away from a boss inside 80 until it is outside 80; out of the pack toward food when enemies are inside 60 and health is under two thirds or just fell fast; sideways off a boss lane; away from the densest side. Empty direction bins are not a dense side and are not a stop.
- While a telegraph is out, the whole line is the sidestep off that circle. Leave any telegraph first was already the lead from 3:20, and Late Blight (45, often from beyond 100) plus Sir Strawman (53) still landed. Distance from the body does not dodge the circle. Do not leave a crowd heading up through a boss minute.
- While a boss is inside 80, away from that boss is the only destination. Do not add a second walk toward food or off the flies. Inside 50 is melee.
- While oil is ticking, the only destination is off the oil, until the ticks stop. Name no food, no boss chase, and no kept heading. A second destination walks back onto the oil.
- If no food is visible, no boss is inside 80, and health is under half, turn away from the side that just dealt damage and keep walking until the hits stop.
- A clear near ring is not safety. A fast drop (about half the bar within 30 seconds, or most of the bar within 10 seconds) leaves only the telegraph, then oil, then away from a boss inside 80, otherwise away from whatever is dealing the damage. That line names no chest and no shrine. Food does not outheal boss circles on a 111 bar.
- A chest and a health fallback in one line send the bot to the chest. The coach is read for a while. When health is falling, a boss is inside 80, or oil is ticking, the line has no chest.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss attacks are aimed at the tile. Step sideways out of the lane.
- A boss inside 80 is a walk directly away until it is outside 80. The circle is still the threat when the boss is well outside 80. This death was Sir Strawman closing from 198 to 133 while the bar sat at 11%, after a 27-point burst at about 48 units.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a low bar. Step out before food or a chest, and do not turn back onto the oil.
- On this Daily: Amusing around 2:00, Colorado Queen around 3:00, The Masher around 4:00, Late Blight around 6:00, Sir Strawman around 7:00, The Fryer King around 8:00 with oil, Great Wireworm around 10:00, The Mandoline around 12:00, Deep Freezer around 14:00, Grater Golem around 16:00, Compost Titan around 17:00. Late Blight is passed once Overgrowth exists. On the longer runs the late chip was Great Wireworm, harvest mice, field rats, and fry-flies. Deep Freezer and Compost Titan are the late circles; mud golems and grease fiends finish a low bar.
- Circles first, then out of oil, then away from a boss inside 80, then out of the pack. Falling health outranks a chest, a shrine, and a gap in the crowd.
