# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run from what happened. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

- Evolutions are the power spike: a weapon at level 8 plus its catalyst, then the next chest, opened while moving and off the densest tiles. The next weapon starts only after that evolution exists, and only when its catalyst is already owned.
- On this Daily the chain is Overgrowth, then Jet Fryer, then Big Fry. Seeker Sprouts to 8, Fertilizer on the first offer, then one chest before Late Blight (~6:00). After Overgrowth exists: Afterburner Fries to 8 with Hot Start already owned, then Small Fry to 8 (Fertilizer is already the catalyst). Until that weapon is 8, every level-up that offers it takes it.
- Until Fertilizer is owned, want is only Fertilizer, Hearty Helping, Gravy Heart, Hash Rush, and Starch Plating. Fertilizer outranks the other four. It stayed on the list all this run and was refused at 2:27, 4:08, 4:16, and 4:32.
- Copy button text exactly. The sustain pick is Hash Rush. The 5:00 consult wrote Rush Hash, so the mark matched nothing.
- The next catalyst waits until the current evolution exists. Hot Start taken before Fertilizer spent the level Overgrowth needed. After the evolution exists, take the next catalyst as soon as it is offered, then that weapon.
- Otherwise take only sustain: Hearty Helping, Gravy Heart, Hash Rush, or Starch Plating. Keep all four on want all run. None were taken; max HP stayed about 111.
- Hold every other weapon and every other passive until the current evolution exists. This run ended Seeker Sprouts 8/8, Locked Gatling 8/8, Chives 5/5, Slow Cooker 4/5, Rotten Spud 3/5, Masher Grip 2/5, Gravy Splash 1/5, Hot Start 1/5. Locked Gatling at 8 made the nearest chest Tater Minigun. Leave a leaked evolution closed.
- After Big Fry, one more pair: Hot Potato to 8 only if Hot Sauce is already owned, then Spicy Hot Potato from a chest already on the way out. If that chest is far or health is falling, leave it. Three evolutions and a full bar beat a fourth inside the swarm.
- Skip chests that evolve nothing, shrines, and any chest inside the swarm.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still with enemies close.
- Order is strict: off a tile where a boss attack will land, then out of hot oil or puddles, then out of the near-ring swarm toward food when health is under two thirds or just fell fast, then sideways off a boss lane, and only then away from the densest side.
- A telegraph under 1.5 seconds overrides the coach line, food, and the crowd. Leave any telegraph first. Never name a one-shot step that is still the plan after the circle is gone.
- A clear near ring is not safety. A modest near ring plus chip damage wipes a naked bar: this run went from 94% to 15% in 30 seconds with 5 to 8 enemies inside 60, hurt by fry-flies at the end. Falling health means leave that pack toward food. The line then drops the chest, the shrine, and the shopping list.
- A fast drop (half the bar within about 30 seconds) means leave toward food now. Food does not outheal the burst by itself.
- The coach is read for a while. Write a line that is still correct a minute later, with the movement order in the first clause. Once health is falling, the whole line is telegraph, oil, then food. The 6:03 line was still the plan at 6:38 and still named chests and Fertilizer.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss attacks are aimed at the tile. Step sideways out of the lane. Distance from the body does not dodge the circle: a Fryer King shot has landed from about 300, and Late Blight has killed from 76 and from about 100.
- A boss body inside 50 is a reason to step away. The circle is still the threat when the boss is well outside 50.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a low bar. Step out before food or a chest. Oil added 12 on this death.
- On this Daily: Amusing around 2:00, The Masher around 4:00, Late Blight around 6:00, Sir Strawman around 7:00, The Fryer King around 8:00 with oil, Great Wireworm around 10:00, The Mandoline around 12:00 with a checker telegraph. Late Blight is the wall without Overgrowth (deaths at 6:17 and 6:38). After 10:00, rat swarms are the health problem; circles are still the first step.
- Hay-bales (33), fry-flies (32), and sizzle-imps (17) did the non-boss damage beside Late Blight (67). Circles first, then out of that pack toward food.
- Falling health outranks a chest, a shrine, and a gap in the crowd.
