# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run from what happened. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

- Evolutions are the power spike: a weapon at level 8 plus its catalyst, then the next chest, opened while the bar is full, moving, and off the densest tiles. Open it on the same beat the pair is ready. A chest carried into the next boss stays closed.
- On this Daily the chain is Overgrowth, then Jet Fryer, then Big Fry. Fertilizer first, Seeker Sprouts to 8, then one chest. Overgrowth around 4:00 (3:43 and 3:59 on the long runs) is what passed Late Blight. This run died at 3:14 with sprouts at 7/8, so take that chest before Colorado Queen (~3:00) when the pair is ready, and otherwise before Late Blight (~6:00).
- Until a consult sees Fertilizer owned, Seeker Sprouts stays out of the sentence and off want. Want is Fertilizer, Gravy Heart, Hearty Helping, Hash Rush, and Starch Plating. Copy button text exactly. The clause is: take Fertilizer only if it is not already owned; if it is already owned, do not take it again; otherwise take Gravy Heart, then Hearty Helping, Hash Rush, or Starch Plating; otherwise reroll. Never write "take X over Gravy Heart" and never write "refuse" without reroll. The 0:30 line stayed up after the pick and Fertilizer went to 3/5. Naming sprouts at 0:30 on the prior run got Fertilizer skipped.
- Once Fertilizer is owned, the named card is Seeker Sprouts until it is 8, Fertilizer leaves the sentence, and want is Seeker Sprouts plus the four sustain cards. While sprouts are below 8, take Gravy Heart whenever it is shown; take another sustain card only when sprouts and Gravy Heart are both absent; otherwise reroll. Sprouts sat at 7/8 from 2:13 until death. Open a chest only once Seeker Sprouts is 8.
- After Overgrowth, take Hot Start once if it is missing, then name only Afterburner Fries until it is 8, then one chest for Jet Fryer. A second level into Hot Start is a leak. If Hot Start is still unowned by 5:00 and a weapon slot is open, name Small Fry until 8, then one chest for Big Fry.
- Gravy Heart outranks the other three sustain cards and may be stacked the whole run. Gravy Heart at 5/5 raised max HP from about 111 to 183 on the run that reached 17:20. Take it whenever it is shown and a passive slot is open, including when the weapon name is missing. Keep all four sustain names on want for the whole run. This run ended near 111 HP with Hash Rush 2/5 and no Gravy Heart; fry-flies plus oil were larger than that bar.
- Six weapons and six passives. After six passives exist, a missing catalyst can never be taken. Once four passives exist, the open slots are for Gravy Heart and the other three sustain cards only. Gravy Splash filled a slot by 1:19 while the line said reroll. It is never a take.
- Hold Tot Toss, Tot Stampede, Chives, Fry Knives, Reset Button, Gravy Splash, Butter Pat, Couch Potato, Frozen Fries, Tailgate Fries, and Slow Cooker. A leak at 8 makes the nearest chest the wrong evolution. Leave that chest closed while the planned weapon can still reach 8.
- If the planned catalyst is absent and one leaked pair is already 8 with its catalyst maxed, open that one chest while the bar is full, health is steady, and the near ring is empty, then walk past every later chest. Stop after that chest.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still while any enemy is inside 60. A heading that is still taking hits is a standstill. This death was fry-flies inside 60 while the near ring looked empty: health went from about 19% to 3% in 30 seconds, with 6 to 9 enemies within 60.
- Order is strict: off a tile where a boss attack will land, then out of hot oil or puddles and stay out until the ticks stop, then away from a boss body inside 50, then out of the pack toward food when enemies are inside 60 and health is under two thirds or just fell fast, then sideways off a boss lane, and only then away from the densest side. Empty direction bins are not a dense side and are not a stop.
- If no food is visible and health is under half, turn away from the side that just dealt damage and keep walking until the hits stop. Holding the last heading from 2:30 walked through fry-flies and oil with no food in sight.
- While oil is ticking, the movement clause has one destination: off the oil, until the ticks stop. It names no food, no boss chase, and no kept heading. A second destination is what walks back onto the oil. Oil did 47 this run.
- A telegraph under 1.5 seconds overrides the coach line, food, and the crowd. Leave any telegraph first. Never name a one-shot step that is still the plan after the circle is gone.
- A clear near ring is not safety. Falling health means walk off the damage toward food, and that line names no chest, shrine, or card. A fast drop (about half the bar within 30 seconds, or most of the bar within 10 seconds) leaves only telegraph, oil, then away from whatever is dealing the damage. Food does not outheal fry-flies. They did 87 this run on a bar of about 111.
- A chest and a health fallback in one line send the bot to the chest. The coach is read for a while. Write a line that is still correct a minute later, with the movement order in the first clause, and a level-up clause that is still correct after the named card is picked. When health is falling or oil is ticking, the line has no chest.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss attacks are aimed at the tile. Step sideways out of the lane. Distance from the body does not dodge the circle.
- A boss body inside 50 is a reason to step away. The circle is still the threat when the boss is well outside 50.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a low bar. Step out before food or a chest, and do not turn back onto the oil. Oil did 47 this run, 15 the run before, and 43 the run before that.
- On this Daily: Amusing around 2:00, Colorado Queen (beetle queen) around 3:00, The Masher around 4:00, Late Blight around 6:00, Sir Strawman around 7:00, The Fryer King around 8:00 with oil, Great Wireworm around 10:00, The Mandoline around 12:00, Deep Freezer around 14:00, Grater Golem around 16:00, Compost Titan around 17:00. Late Blight is passed once Overgrowth exists. During Colorado Queen the lethal sources were fry-flies and oil; her own damage was 7. On the longer runs the late chip was Great Wireworm, harvest mice, field rats, and fry-flies. Deep Freezer and Compost Titan are the late circles; mud golems and grease fiends finish a low bar.
- Circles first, then out of oil, then out of that pack toward food. Falling health outranks a chest, a shrine, and a gap in the crowd.
