# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run from what happened. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

- Evolutions are the main power spike: a base weapon at level 8 plus its catalyst evolves at the next chest. Commit to two or three weapons and level them to 8 rather than spreading picks.
- Take the catalyst passive for a weapon you are already levelling as soon as it is offered.
- Max HP and armor matter more on Nightmare than on Easy; take them when nothing pairs.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own, so staying alive is the job: keep moving, never stand still with enemies close.
- Move away from the densest side, not toward open space next to a crowd.
- Pick up food when health drops below half, and open chests: they carry the evolutions.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss sweeps are long straight lanes: step sideways out of the lane, not along it.
- On the current Daily the Ketchup Kraken arrives around 18:00; earlier runs lost most of their health between 18:00 and 19:30 to its bullets, puddles and lanes. Enter that phase with full health and keep distance.
- Burning ground and damaging puddles hurt over time; step out of them.

## Lessons

- 2026-11-06, Easy Daily: the best run reached 19:30 with Laya after switching to per-direction danger questions; deaths came from the 18:00 boss phase.
