# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

- Evolutions are the power spike: a weapon at 8 plus its catalyst, then the next chest, opened while the bar is full, moving, and off circles, oil, and puddles. Open it on the beat the pair is ready. A chest carried into the next boss stays closed.
- On this Daily the chain is Overgrowth, then Jet Fryer, then Big Fry. Take Fertilizer once and Seeker Sprouts to 8, then one chest before Late Blight (~6:00). Overgrowth around 4:00 is what passed that minute. Sprouts short of 8 die there: 6:37 at 6/8, and the wide run at 6:07 with 3/8.
- Six weapons without an evolution stay on the short wall. The wide runs evolved nothing and died at 6:07 (six weapons, level 17) and 3:21 (three weapons, level 9). One- and two-weapon endings are seven runs, 3:14 to 6:38. Three-weapon endings are four runs, 3:21 to 7:18. Six-weapon endings run 6:07 to 17:20, and the ones that passed 8:00 had an evolution first. Take no new weapon before Seeker Sprouts is 8. An unplanned weapon already at 8 makes the nearest chest the wrong evolution: leave that chest closed while the planned weapon can still reach 8. Tailgate Fries at 8/8 by 3:04, chest shut, no catalyst, did not move the death.
- After Overgrowth the long-run weapons stay legal, Fry Knives included. The 17:20 end inventory was Tot Gatling 1/1, Tot Stampede 8/8, Hungry Sprouts 1/1, Fry Knives 8/8, Tailgate Fries 8/8, Reset Button 6/8, with Gravy Splash, Butter Pat, Chives, and Couch Potato at 5/5 beside Gravy Heart 5/5 and Fertilizer 1/5.
- A name in want is taken on every offer until it leaves want. The sentence does not beat the mark. Copy button text exactly. Never write refuse without reroll. On the 3:21 wide run, Frozen Fries was in want and leveled at 2:05; Small Fry was on that screen and was skipped because it was not in want.
- Fertilizer is in want only while unowned, and it leaves want at the first stack, before the next card, not at the next consult. A second Fertilizer level is a leak. A 0:30 mark held to 2:00 put Fertilizer at 4/5, and a sprout on those screens lost to the mark. The 17:20 run held Fertilizer at 1/5. The 7:18 row was Fertilizer 5/5 with no Gravy Heart.
- Until Fertilizer is owned: if it is not owned take Fertilizer; once it is owned, if Seeker Sprouts is below 8 take Seeker Sprouts; when Seeker Sprouts is absent take Gravy Heart; when both are absent and a reroll remains, reroll; when no reroll remains take Butter Pat, else Gravy Splash, else Chives, else Couch Potato.
- Once Fertilizer is owned and sprouts are below 8, want is Seeker Sprouts and Gravy Heart. Take Seeker Sprouts when offered; when it is absent take Gravy Heart; when both are absent and a reroll remains, reroll; when no reroll remains use that same Butter Pat, Gravy Splash, Chives, Couch Potato order. Keep writing the reroll. Leave leaked names out of the line. Empty rerolls took Golden Fry, Peel Out, and Potato Magnet on the 6:37 run, Spud Sneakers, Sprouting Eyes, and Butter Pat on the 6:07 wide run, and Mash Finisher, GPU, and Bacon Bits on the 3:21 wide run.
- Hearty Helping, Hash Rush, Hot Start, and Starch Plating stay off want until Gravy Heart is owned and Overgrowth exists. Starch Plating before Overgrowth stalled sprouts at 5/8 twice. Butter Pat, Gravy Splash, Chives, and Couch Potato stay off want so the mark cannot stack them; they are only the pick when no reroll remains.
- Six passives can be full before 4:00. A missing Gravy Heart can never be taken after that. An owned Gravy Heart stacks in its slot up to 5/5; keep it in want. Gravy Heart at 5/5 raised max HP from about 111 to 183. Runs that never owned it stayed at 111. Wide-run oil of 109 and 140 erases a 111 bar.
- After Overgrowth, take Hot Start once if it is missing, then name only Afterburner Fries until it is 8, then one chest for Jet Fryer. If Hot Start is still unowned by 5:00 and a weapon slot is open, name Small Fry until 8, then one chest for Big Fry. After that, stack Gravy Heart, then Starch Plating, Hearty Helping, and Hash Rush, and take the long-run weapons.
- Gravy Heart outranks the other sustain cards. Stack it whenever it is owned, including when the weapon card is absent and when the other passive slots are full.
- Reroll Mash Finisher, Lean Lunch, Slow Cooker, Sour Cream, Sprouting Eyes, GPU, Packed Pan, Spry Sprout, Spud Sneakers, Rotten Spud, Masher Grip, Golden Fry, Peel Out, Potato Magnet, and Bacon Bits whenever a reroll remains. When none remains and Butter Pat, Gravy Splash, Chives, or Couch Potato is on the screen, take that card in that order.
- If the planned catalyst is absent and one other pair is already 8 with its catalyst maxed, open that one chest while the bar is full, health is steady, the near ring is empty, and no puddle is ticking, then walk past every later chest.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still while any enemy is inside 60. A heading whose condition is already true is a standstill. Away-until-outside-80 gives no heading once the boss is outside 80.
- The line names one destination, then one level-up clause that stays correct after the named card is picked. The coach is read until the next consult, so the destination has to stay true when a circle or a puddle appears. Write only the first destination that applies. A second heading walks back onto the tile just left. The wide-run lines named the oil exit and then another heading, and hot oil led the damage: 140 at 6:07, 109 at 3:21.
- During a boss minute, write only the first that applies: step sideways off any circle you are on; if you are in hot oil or any damaging puddle, step off it and stay off it until the ticks stop, with no other heading in the line; otherwise walk the thinner side that is not toward the boss. Copy that single clause for the whole minute, including consults where no circle is showing and the boss is outside 50. Food is not a destination in that minute.
- Straight away from the boss gives the wrong heading. Walk the thinner side. Marks and puddles land from well outside melee. The queen circle landed at 0.9s from 65 away. The wide runs did not close on her. Her chip was 2 on both, and fry-flies were 2 on the 6:07 run. Earlier deaths that walked off the flies onto her took the fly chip (94 and 72). Keep the no-close heading.
- Outside a boss minute, only the first that applies: off a circle; out of hot oil or a damaging puddle until the ticks stop, with no other heading; if about a third of the bar just dropped, away from the side that dealt it; away from a fry-fly or sizzle-imp pack while it is the damage; toward food only when enemies are inside 60, health is under two thirds, and the damage is not flies or imps; away from the densest side. Empty direction bins are not a dense side and are not a stop.
- A clear near ring still dies, and a full bar still dies. Food does not outheal fry-flies, sizzle-imps, or a puddle. A fast drop names no chest and no shrine.
- A chest and a health fallback in one line send the bot to the chest. When health is falling, a boss is inside 80, a circle is out, or oil or a puddle is ticking, the line has no chest.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss attacks are aimed at the tile. Step sideways out of the lane. When a circle is the threat, that sidestep is the whole boss-minute line, including consults where no circle is showing yet.
- A boss inside 80 means do not close distance. It does not replace the sidestep, the puddle exit, or the thinner-side heading. Inside 50 is melee. The circle, the oil, and the puddle stay the threat when the boss is well outside melee.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a full bar. Step out before food or a chest, and do not turn back onto the tile. The 6:07 wide run was full at the 6:03 telegraph and dead at 6:07 (hot oil 140, sizzle-imps 73, hay-bales 19, Late Blight 11). The 3:21 wide death's ledger was hot oil 109 and Colorado Queen 2. The 6:37 death stood on a puddle the line did not name: Masher 33, hot oil 31, Late Blight 30, snow mold 15, fry-flies 13.
- On this Daily: Amusing around 2:00, Colorado Queen around 3:00, The Masher around 4:00, Late Blight around 6:00, Sir Strawman around 7:00, The Fryer King around 8:00 with oil, Great Wireworm around 10:00, The Mandoline around 12:00, Deep Freezer around 14:00, Grater Golem around 16:00, Compost Titan around 17:00. Late Blight is passed once Overgrowth exists. Without it, oil and sizzle-imps wipe that minute from a full bar. Queen-minute oil ended the 3:21 wide run while her chip stayed at 2. On the longer runs the late chip was Great Wireworm, harvest mice, field rats, and fry-flies. Deep Freezer and Compost Titan are the late circles; mud golems and grease fiends finish a low bar.
- Circles first, then out of oil and puddles, then the thinner side not toward a boss inside 80, then away from the swarm that is dealing the damage. Falling health outranks a chest, a shrine, food, and a gap in the crowd.
