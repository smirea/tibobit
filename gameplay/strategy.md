# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run from what happened. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

- Evolutions are the power spike: a base weapon at level 8 plus its catalyst evolves at the next chest. Commit to two weapons that share a catalyst already owned, and open that chest while moving as soon as it is off the densest tiles.
- Take a ready evolution on the way out of the pack. Overgrowth sat one chest away from 2:00 until about 7:00 while health never recovered, so the spike arrived after the bleed had already decided the run.
- The second weapon uses a catalyst already owned. Fertilizer was done at 2:00, so Small Fry or Seed Potato could evolve next. Tailgate Fries and Grab Bag reached 5/8 with Butter Pat and Sprouting Eyes still unowned.
- Level only those two weapons, their catalyst, and sustain (max HP, armor, Gravy Heart, Hash Rush). Tot Toss, Tot Stampede, and Reset Button were wasted levels.
- When the catalyst is not on offer, take sustain instead of another weapon. A third weapon without its catalyst does not evolve.
- Max HP, armor, Gravy Heart, and Hash Rush come as soon as the pair is chosen. This run was still near 30 health from 3:31 through the death.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Stay alive: keep moving, and never stand still with enemies close.
- Movement order is strict: step off a tile where a boss attack will land, then out of hot oil or puddles, then away from a boss inside 50 units, then toward food when health is under two thirds, and only then away from the densest side.
- An empty near ring (0 enemies within 60) means the pack is not the threat. Clear the telegraph and the oil underfoot before walking away from a distant crowd.
- On Nightmare, fry-flies bleed faster than late food replaces. From 2:30 to 3:31 health fell from full to 37/111. Grab food whenever health is under two thirds, and do not stop on a chest inside the swarm.
- When health stays under a third for a full minute, leave the hazard and take the ready chest on the way out. Orbiting for a heal that is not arriving lost this run.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss sweeps are long straight lanes. Step sideways out of the lane. Distance does not dodge an aimed shot: The Fryer King still dealt damage from about 300 units.
- A telegraph under 1.5 seconds overrides the coach line about the pack. This run died at 8:09 standing on a Fryer King attack with 0.7 seconds left and 9% health.
- Sir Strawman closed to 18 units and hit for about 34, more than the health left. A boss inside 50 units is the movement target.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a low bar. Step out of them before food or a chest. Oil plus the Fryer King ended the run.
- On this Daily, fry-flies were the early damage (58 by 8:09), ahead of every boss. The Fryer King is up by 8:00 with oil and telegraphs. Survive that phase before saving care for a later boss.

## Lessons

- 2026-11-06, Easy Daily: the best run reached 19:30 with Laya after switching to per-direction danger questions; deaths came from the 18:00 boss phase.
- 2026-11-07, Nightmare Daily: died at 8:09 on a Fryer King telegraph in hot oil at 9% health. Overgrowth was the right first evolution, but the chest waited from 2:00 until about 7:00, fry-flies bled health from full to a third by 3:31 and it never came back, and levels leaked into weapons with no catalyst.
