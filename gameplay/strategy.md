# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run from what happened. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

- Evolutions are the power spike: a weapon at level 8 plus its catalyst, then the next chest, opened while the bar is full, moving, and off the densest tiles. Open it on the same beat the pair is ready. A chest carried into the next boss stays closed.
- On this Daily the chain is Overgrowth, then Jet Fryer, then Big Fry. Fertilizer first, Seeker Sprouts to 8, then one chest before Late Blight (~6:00). Overgrowth around 4:00 (3:59 this run) is what passed Late Blight.
- Until Fertilizer is owned, the named card is only Fertilizer. Want is Fertilizer, Gravy Heart, Hearty Helping, Hash Rush, and Starch Plating. Copy button text exactly. The advice sentence does not contain Seeker Sprouts. Naming sprouts at 0:30 got Fertilizer skipped at 1:10 and 1:57.
- The level-up clause is always this shape: take the one named card if it is shown; otherwise take Gravy Heart, then Hearty Helping, Hash Rush, or Starch Plating; otherwise reroll. Never write "take X over Gravy Heart" and never write "refuse" without reroll. That ranking refused Gravy Heart again while Frozen Fries, Tailgate Fries, Tot Toss, and Reset Button filled the bar. Reroll is in the clause from the first level-up; it was first said at 11:00, after six weapons existed.
- With Fertilizer owned, the named card is Seeker Sprouts until it is 8, and the only other takes are the four sustain cards, then reroll. Open a chest only once Seeker Sprouts is 8.
- After Overgrowth, take Hot Start once if it is missing, then name only Afterburner Fries until it is 8, then one chest for Jet Fryer. A second level into Hot Start is a leak; this run it maxed. If Hot Start is still unowned by 5:00 and a weapon slot is open, name Small Fry until 8, then one chest for Big Fry.
- Gravy Heart outranks the other three sustain cards. Gravy Heart at 5/5 raised max HP from about 111 to 183 on the run that reached 17:20. Take it whenever it is shown and a passive slot is open, including when the weapon name is missing. Keep all four sustain names on want for the whole run.
- Six weapons and six passives. At 10:00 the bar was full: Overgrowth, Afterburner Fries 3/8, Frozen Fries 8/8, Tailgate Fries 6/8, Tot Toss 5/8, Reset Button 1/8. Afterburner Fries was 5/8 at 11:00 and Jet Fryer never happened. After six passives exist, a missing catalyst can never be taken. Chives, Slow Cooker, Gravy Splash, and Hot Start maxed beside Fertilizer, so the last slot never became Gravy Heart. Once four passives exist, the open slots are for Gravy Heart and the other three sustain cards only.
- Hold Tot Toss, Tot Stampede, Chives, Fry Knives, Reset Button, Gravy Splash, Butter Pat, Couch Potato, Frozen Fries, Tailgate Fries, and Slow Cooker. A leak at 8 makes the nearest chest the wrong evolution. Frozen Fries was 8/8 by 10:00 while Afterburner Fries was 3/8, and Tot Toss was 5/8 on maxed Chives. Leave that chest closed while the planned weapon can still reach 8.
- If the planned catalyst is absent and one leaked pair is already 8 with its catalyst maxed, open that one chest while the bar is full, health is steady, and the near ring is empty, then walk past every later chest. Stop after that chest.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still while any enemy is inside 60. This death was a standstill: health was 100% at 11:08, then field rats and harvest mice took 79% in under ten seconds with 11 enemies within 60.
- Order is strict: off a tile where a boss attack will land, then out of hot oil or puddles, then away from a boss body inside 50, then out of the pack toward food when enemies are inside 60 and health is under two thirds or just fell fast, then sideways off a boss lane, and only then away from the densest side. Empty direction bins are not a dense side and are not a stop. If the bins are empty, keep the last heading or walk toward food. Zero velocity is never the line.
- A telegraph under 1.5 seconds overrides the coach line, food, and the crowd. Leave any telegraph first. Never name a one-shot step that is still the plan after the circle is gone.
- A clear near ring is not safety. Falling health means walk toward food, and that line names no chest, shrine, or card. A fast drop (about half the bar within 30 seconds, or most of the bar within 10 seconds) leaves only telegraph, oil, then food. Food does not outheal the burst; standing in the pack is the burst.
- A chest and a health fallback in one line send the bot to the chest. The 11:00 chest line was still the plan through the drop that killed at 11:24. The coach is read for a while. Write a line that is still correct a minute later, with the movement order in the first clause. When health is falling, the line has no chest.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss attacks are aimed at the tile. Step sideways out of the lane. Distance from the body does not dodge the circle.
- A boss body inside 50 is a reason to step away. The circle is still the threat when the boss is well outside 50.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a low bar. Step out before food or a chest. Oil did 15 this run and 43 the run before.
- On this Daily: Amusing around 2:00, The Masher around 4:00, Late Blight around 6:00, Sir Strawman around 7:00, The Fryer King around 8:00 with oil, Great Wireworm around 10:00, The Mandoline around 12:00, Deep Freezer around 14:00, Grater Golem around 16:00, Compost Titan around 17:00. Late Blight is passed once Overgrowth exists. After 8:00 the chip damage was Great Wireworm 83, harvest mice 83, field rats 76, fry-flies 38, Fryer King 12, and oil 15. The kill was field rats and harvest mice while standing still, not a boss circle. Deep Freezer and Compost Titan are the late circles; mud golems and grease fiends finish a low bar.
- Circles first, then out of that pack toward food. Falling health outranks a chest, a shrine, and a gap in the crowd.
