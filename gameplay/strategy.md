# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run from what happened. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

- Evolutions are the power spike: a weapon at 8 plus its catalyst, then the next chest, opened while the bar is full, moving, and off circles, oil, and puddles. Open it on the beat the pair is ready. A chest carried into the next boss stays closed.
- On this Daily the chain is Overgrowth, then Jet Fryer, then Big Fry. Take Fertilizer once and Seeker Sprouts to 8, then one chest before Late Blight (~6:00). Overgrowth around 4:00 passed Late Blight on the runs that reached it. Sprouts still short of 8 at that boss die there: this run ended at 6:37 with Seeker Sprouts 6/8.
- The longest end inventory was Tot Gatling 1/1, Tot Stampede 8/8, Hungry Sprouts 1/1, Fry Knives 8/8, Tailgate Fries 8/8, Reset Button 6/8, with Gravy Splash, Butter Pat, Chives, and Couch Potato at 5/5 beside Gravy Heart 5/5 and Fertilizer 1/5. One- and two-weapon endings are seven runs from 3:14 to 6:38. Three-weapon endings run 6:17 to 7:18. Five- and six-weapon endings run 8:09 to 17:20. Those weapons and passives stay legal after Overgrowth. Feed the planned pair first. An unplanned weapon already at 8 makes the nearest chest the wrong evolution: leave that chest closed while the planned weapon can still reach 8.
- A name in want is taken on every offer until it leaves want. The sentence does not beat the mark. Copy button text exactly. Never write refuse without reroll.
- Fertilizer is in want only while unowned, and it leaves want at the first stack, before the next card, not at the next consult. A second Fertilizer level is a leak. The 0:30 mark lasted until 2:00 and Fertilizer reached 4/5, so a sprout on those screens lost to the mark. The 17:20 run held Fertilizer at 1/5. The 7:18 row was Fertilizer 5/5 with no Gravy Heart.
- Until Fertilizer is owned, the clause is: if Fertilizer is not owned take Fertilizer; once it is owned, if Seeker Sprouts is below 8 take Seeker Sprouts; when Seeker Sprouts is absent take Gravy Heart; when both are absent and a reroll remains, reroll; when no reroll remains take Butter Pat, else Gravy Splash, else Chives, else Couch Potato. Take no new weapon before Seeker Sprouts is 8. The old fill clause had already leaked Grab Bag and Fry Knives by 2:00; both were still 3/8 at Late Blight.
- Once Fertilizer is owned and sprouts are below 8, want is Seeker Sprouts and Gravy Heart. Take Seeker Sprouts when offered; when it is absent take Gravy Heart; when both are absent and a reroll remains, reroll; when no reroll remains use that same Butter Pat, Gravy Splash, Chives, Couch Potato order. Keep writing the reroll. Leave leaked names out of the line. Empty rerolls from 2:00 to 6:37 still took Golden Fry 2/5, Peel Out 1/5, and Potato Magnet 1/5. The 4:26 screen was Mash Finisher, Golden Fry, and Butter Pat.
- Hearty Helping, Hash Rush, Hot Start, and Starch Plating stay off want until Gravy Heart is owned and Overgrowth exists. Starch Plating before Overgrowth stalled sprouts at 5/8 twice. Butter Pat, Gravy Splash, Chives, and Couch Potato stay off want so the mark cannot stack them; they are only the pick when no reroll remains.
- Six passives can be full before 4:00. A missing Gravy Heart can never be taken after that. An owned Gravy Heart stacks in its slot up to 5/5; keep it in want. Gravy Heart at 5/5 raised max HP from about 111 to 183 on the 17:20 run. This run never owned it, and the bar stayed 111.
- After Overgrowth, take Hot Start once if it is missing, then name only Afterburner Fries until it is 8, then one chest for Jet Fryer. If Hot Start is still unowned by 5:00 and a weapon slot is open, name Small Fry until 8, then one chest for Big Fry. After that, stack Gravy Heart, then Starch Plating, Hearty Helping, and Hash Rush, and take the long-run weapons, Fry Knives included.
- Gravy Heart outranks the other sustain cards. Stack it whenever it is owned, including when the weapon card is absent and when the other passive slots are full.
- Reroll Mash Finisher, Lean Lunch, Slow Cooker, Sour Cream, Sprouting Eyes, GPU, Packed Pan, Spry Sprout, Spud Sneakers, Rotten Spud, Masher Grip, Golden Fry, Peel Out, and Potato Magnet whenever a reroll remains. When none remains and Butter Pat, Gravy Splash, Chives, or Couch Potato is on the screen, take that card in that order.
- If the planned catalyst is absent and one other pair is already 8 with its catalyst maxed, open that one chest while the bar is full, health is steady, the near ring is empty, and no puddle is ticking, then walk past every later chest.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still while any enemy is inside 60. A heading whose condition is already true is a standstill. Away-until-outside-80 gives no heading once the boss is outside 80.
- The line names one destination, then one level-up clause that stays correct after the named card is picked. The coach is read until the next consult, so the destination has to stay true when a circle or a puddle appears.
- During any boss minute the destination is this, in order: step sideways off any circle you are on; if you are in hot oil or any damaging puddle, step off it and stay off it until the ticks stop; otherwise walk toward the thinner side that is not toward the boss. Copy that for the whole minute, including consults where no circle is showing and the boss is outside 50. Food is not a destination in that minute. Health held at 77/111 through Colorado Queen. At 6:35 the line named only hot oil, and Tibo was still standing in a damaging puddle while health fell from 57% to 28% with Late Blight 69 away, then to 1% at 6:37.
- Straight away from the boss gives the wrong heading. Walk the thinner side, not toward the boss. Marks and puddles land from well outside melee. On the prior queen death her circle landed at 0.9s from 65 away.
- Outside a boss minute, and only the first that applies: off a circle; out of hot oil or a damaging puddle until the ticks stop; if about a third of the bar just dropped, away from the side that dealt it; away from a fry-fly pack while it is the damage; toward food only when enemies are inside 60, health is under two thirds, and the damage is not flies; away from the densest side. Empty direction bins are not a dense side and are not a stop.
- A clear near ring is not safety. Food does not outheal fry-flies or a puddle. A fast drop names no chest and no shrine.
- A chest and a health fallback in one line send the bot to the chest. When health is falling, a boss is inside 80, a circle is out, or oil or a puddle is ticking, the line has no chest.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss attacks are aimed at the tile. Step sideways out of the lane. Clause one stays the sidestep for the whole boss minute, including consults where no circle is showing yet.
- A boss inside 80 means do not close distance. It does not replace the sidestep, the puddle exit, or the thinner-side heading. Inside 50 is melee. The circle and the puddle stay the threat when the boss is well outside melee.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a low bar. Step out before food or a chest, and do not turn back onto the tile. A second destination walks back onto it. This run the damage was The Masher 33, hot oil 31, Late Blight 30, snow mold 15, fry-flies 13.
- On this Daily: Amusing around 2:00, Colorado Queen around 3:00, The Masher around 4:00, Late Blight around 6:00, Sir Strawman around 7:00, The Fryer King around 8:00 with oil, Great Wireworm around 10:00, The Mandoline around 12:00, Deep Freezer around 14:00, Grater Golem around 16:00, Compost Titan around 17:00. Queen plus fry-flies is the early wipe while sprouts are short of 8. Late Blight is passed once Overgrowth exists. On the longer runs the late chip was Great Wireworm, harvest mice, field rats, and fry-flies. Deep Freezer and Compost Titan are the late circles; mud golems and grease fiends finish a low bar.
- Circles first, then out of oil and puddles, then the thinner side not toward a boss inside 80, then away from the swarm that is dealing the damage. Falling health outranks a chest, a shrine, food, and a gap in the crowd.
