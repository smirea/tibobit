# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run from what happened. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

- Evolutions are the power spike: a weapon at level 8 plus its catalyst, then the next chest, opened while the bar is full, moving, and off the densest tiles. Open it on the same beat the pair is ready. A chest carried into the next boss stays closed.
- On this Daily the chain is Overgrowth, then Jet Fryer, then Big Fry. Fertilizer once, Seeker Sprouts to 8, then one chest before Late Blight (~6:00). Overgrowth around 4:00 passed Late Blight on the long runs. Colorado Queen (~3:00) is the gate before that: sprouts still at 5/8 with a full passive row dies there.
- A name in want is taken every time it is offered until the next consult. The once-only sentence does not beat that mark. Naming Seeker Sprouts before Fertilizer is owned skips Fertilizer. Until a consult shows Fertilizer owned, leave want empty. Copy button text exactly. The clause has to stay true across the gap: if Fertilizer is not owned, take Fertilizer; if Fertilizer is owned and Seeker Sprouts is below 8, take Seeker Sprouts; when Seeker Sprouts is absent, take Gravy Heart; otherwise reroll. Never write a line that takes another card over Gravy Heart, and never write refuse without reroll.
- Once a consult shows Fertilizer owned, Fertilizer leaves the sentence and stays off want. While sprouts are below 8, want is Seeker Sprouts and Gravy Heart. Take Seeker Sprouts when offered; when it is absent take Gravy Heart; otherwise reroll. Prose reroll is not a lock. From 2:17 to 3:36 sprouts stayed 5/8 while Sour Cream, Sprouting Eyes, Starch Plating, GPU, and Fertilizer (to 2/5) were taken under a line that already said otherwise reroll. Keep writing the reroll. Leave the leaked names out of the line.
- Hearty Helping, Hash Rush, Hot Start, and Starch Plating stay off want until Gravy Heart is owned and Overgrowth exists. Starch Plating before Overgrowth has stalled sprouts at 5/8 twice (4/5 at 3:16, 3/5 at 3:36).
- Six passives can be full before 4:00. A missing Gravy Heart can never be taken after that. An owned Gravy Heart still stacks in its own slot up to 5/5; keep it in want. Gravy Heart at 5/5 raised max HP from about 111 to 183 on the 17:20 run. One level left this bar near 125, and fry-flies did 94 to it. Sir Strawman did about 27 in three seconds on a 12/111 bar.
- After Overgrowth, take Hot Start once if it is missing, then name only Afterburner Fries until it is 8, then one chest for Jet Fryer. A second Hot Start is a leak. Any Hot Start before Overgrowth is a leaked slot. If Hot Start is still unowned by 5:00 and a weapon slot is open, name Small Fry until 8, then one chest for Big Fry. After Overgrowth and after Gravy Heart is owned, stack Gravy Heart, then Starch Plating, Hearty Helping, and Hash Rush.
- Gravy Heart outranks the other sustain cards. Stack it whenever it is owned, including when the weapon card is absent and when the other passive slots are full.
- Six weapons and six passives. An open slot goes to Gravy Heart until it is owned, then to the next planned catalyst, then Hearty Helping, Hash Rush, and, after Overgrowth, Starch Plating. Gravy Splash is never a take.
- Hold Tot Toss, Tot Stampede, Chives, Fry Knives, Reset Button, Gravy Splash, Butter Pat, Couch Potato, Frozen Fries, Tailgate Fries, Slow Cooker, Locked Gatling, Grab Bag, GPU, Packed Pan, Sour Cream, and Sprouting Eyes. A leak at 8 makes the nearest chest the wrong evolution. Leave that chest closed while the planned weapon can still reach 8.
- If the planned catalyst is absent and one leaked pair is already 8 with its catalyst maxed, open that one chest while the bar is full, health is steady, and the near ring is empty, then walk past every later chest. Stop after that chest.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still while any enemy is inside 60. A heading whose condition is already true is a standstill. Away-until-outside-80 gives no heading once the boss is outside 80.
- The line names one destination, then one level-up clause that stays correct after the named card is picked. The coach is read for a while, so the destination has to stay true when a circle appears before the next consult.
- During any boss minute the destination is this, in order: step sideways off any circle you are on; if hot oil is ticking, stay off it until the ticks stop; otherwise walk toward the thinner side that is not toward the boss. Copy that. A menu of telegraph, oil, boss, and crowd is four plans.
- Straight away from the boss is not that destination. The 3:36 line was only that, and it was still the plan when Colorado Queen's circle landed at 0.9s from 65 away. Fry-flies did 94, beetle-brood 36, crumb-ants 21, and the queen 12. That heading stayed in the flies. The 3:16 death is the other edge: walking off the flies closed on her. Thinner side, not toward her. Distance from her body does not dodge the circle; marks also landed from 84.
- Outside a boss minute, and only the first that applies: off a circle; out of hot oil until the ticks stop; out of the pack toward food when enemies are inside 60 and health is under two thirds or just fell fast; away from the side that just dealt damage when health is under half; away from the densest side. Empty direction bins are not a dense side and are not a stop.
- A clear near ring is not safety. A fast drop (about half the bar within 30 seconds, or most of the bar within 10 seconds) uses the same order: circle, oil, thinner side not toward a boss inside 80, then away from whatever is dealing the damage. That line names no chest and no shrine. Food does not outheal the fly pack: health went from 86% to 5% in 30 seconds.
- A chest and a health fallback in one line send the bot to the chest. When health is falling, a boss is inside 80, a circle is out, or oil is ticking, the line has no chest.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss attacks are aimed at the tile. Step sideways out of the lane. Clause one stays the sidestep for the whole boss minute, including consults where no circle is showing yet.
- A boss inside 80 means do not close distance. It does not replace the sidestep or the thinner-side heading. Inside 50 is melee; the circle is still the threat when she is well outside melee.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a low bar. Step out before food or a chest, and do not turn back onto the oil. A second destination walks back onto the oil.
- On this Daily: Amusing around 2:00, Colorado Queen around 3:00, The Masher around 4:00, Late Blight around 6:00, Sir Strawman around 7:00, The Fryer King around 8:00 with oil, Great Wireworm around 10:00, The Mandoline around 12:00, Deep Freezer around 14:00, Grater Golem around 16:00, Compost Titan around 17:00. Queen plus fry-flies is the early wipe when sprouts are below 8. Late Blight is passed once Overgrowth exists. On the longer runs the late chip was Great Wireworm, harvest mice, field rats, and fry-flies. Deep Freezer and Compost Titan are the late circles; mud golems and grease fiends finish a low bar.
- Circles first, then out of oil, then the thinner side not toward a boss inside 80, then out of the pack. Falling health outranks a chest, a shrine, and a gap in the crowd.
