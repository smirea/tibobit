# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

During a boss minute it stays closed; the heading that minute is the hazard or the thinner side. Put the evolution name in want only on the consult that opens the chest. Two ready pairs: open Thousand Fries if Fry Knives and Salt Shaker are ready, otherwise Hungry Sprouts, otherwise the other pair. One name only.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still while any enemy is inside 60. A heading whose condition is already true is a standstill. The line is read until the next consult, so it has to stay true after the circle expires. From 16:02 to 18:24 the line was a circle sidestep while nothing was underfoot or the circles were beside you. That standstill met the titan.
- One destination, then one level-up clause that stays correct after the named card is picked. Write only the first destination that applies. A second heading walks back onto the tile just left. Wide-run oil was 140 at 6:07 and 109 at 3:21 under an exit plus another heading. The record took 22 oil.
- On a circle, in hot oil, or in a damaging puddle, the line is only that exit. Nearby, beside you, and nothing underfoot are not an exit. Already clear, walk the thinner side that is not toward a boss inside 80, and keep that heading for the rest of the minute. Two bosses up: the thinner side toward neither. Food and chests are not destinations in that minute.
- Straight away from the boss is the wrong heading. Walk the thinner side, and do not close. The queen circle landed at 0.9s from 65 away. Deaths that walked off the flies onto her took the fly chip. Her chip was 2 when the line did not close.
- Outside a boss minute, the first that applies: off a circle; out of hot oil or a damaging puddle until the ticks stop, with no other heading; if about a third of the bar just dropped, away from the side that dealt it; away from grease fiends, field rats, ice imps, fry-flies, or sizzle-imps while they are the damage; toward food only when enemies are inside 60, health is under two thirds, and the damage is not that swarm; away from the densest side. Empty direction bins are not a dense side and are not a stop.
- Food does not outheal grease fiends, field rats, ice imps, fry-flies, sizzle-imps, or a puddle. A fast drop names no chest and no shrine. Field rats were 170 on the standing 15:22 line and 26 on this run while the thinner side was walked. Grease fiends were 105 beside Compost Titan's 144.
- A chest and a health fallback in one line send the bot to the chest. When health is falling, a boss is inside 80, a circle is underfoot, or oil or a puddle is ticking, the line has no chest.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss attacks are aimed at the tile. On the circle, the sidestep is the whole line. Already clear, the thinner side is the whole line, because the consult does not fire again when the telegraph ends.
- A boss inside 80 means do not close. It does not replace an exit or the thinner-side walk. Inside 50 is melee. Circles, oil, and puddles stay the threat from outside melee. This run kept early bosses past 100: Masher 9, Fryer King 14, Wireworm 28, Grater 22.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a full bar. Step out before food or a chest, and stay off that tile.
- On this Daily: Amusing around 2:00, Colorado Queen around 3:00, The Masher around 4:00, Late Blight around 6:00, Sir Strawman around 7:00, The Fryer King around 8:00 with oil, Great Wireworm around 10:00, The Mandoline around 12:00, Deep Freezer around 14:00, Grater Golem around 16:00, Compost Titan around 17:00, Ketchup Kraken around 18:00 while Compost Titan is still alive. Walking the thinner side got through Deep Freezer's rats. Titan circles, grease fiends, and the Kraken overlap erased a full 111 bar at 18:24.
- On a hazard, exit it. Already clear, walk the thinner side not toward a boss inside 80, else away from the swarm that is dealing the damage. Falling health outranks a chest, a shrine, food, and a gap in the crowd.
