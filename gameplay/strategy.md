# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run from what happened. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

- Evolutions are the power spike: a weapon at 8 plus its catalyst, then the next chest, opened while the bar is full, moving, and off the densest tiles. Open it on the beat the pair is ready. A chest carried into the next boss stays closed.
- On this Daily the chain is Overgrowth, then Jet Fryer, then Big Fry. Fertilizer once, Seeker Sprouts to 8, one chest before Late Blight (~6:00). Overgrowth around 4:00 passed Late Blight on the runs that reached it. Colorado Queen (~3:00) kills sprouts still short of 8; this run died there at 6/8.
- The longest end inventory was Tot Gatling 1/1, Tot Stampede 8/8, Hungry Sprouts 1/1, Fry Knives 8/8, Tailgate Fries 8/8, Reset Button 6/8, with Gravy Splash, Butter Pat, Chives, and Couch Potato at 5/5 beside Gravy Heart 5/5. One- and two-weapon endings are six runs from 3:14 to 6:38. Those long-run weapons and passives stay legal. Feed the planned pair first. An unplanned weapon already at 8 makes the nearest chest the wrong evolution: leave that chest closed while the planned weapon can still reach 8.
- A name in want is taken every time it is offered until the next consult. The sentence does not beat the mark. Copy button text exactly. Never write refuse without reroll. Once Fertilizer is owned, never write a line that takes another card over Gravy Heart.
- Until a consult shows Fertilizer owned, want is Fertilizer only. Empty want from 0:30 to 2:00 took Mash Finisher, Lean Lunch, Slow Cooker, and Sour Cream; sprouts were still 4/8 at 2:13 with one slot left. Naming Seeker Sprouts before Fertilizer is owned skips Fertilizer. The clause still has to stay true after the card is picked: if Fertilizer is not owned, take Fertilizer; if Fertilizer is owned and Seeker Sprouts is below 8, take Seeker Sprouts; when Seeker Sprouts is absent, take Gravy Heart; when both are absent, fill an empty weapon slot; otherwise reroll.
- Once a consult shows Fertilizer owned, it leaves want on that consult. While sprouts are below 8, want is Seeker Sprouts and Gravy Heart. Take Seeker Sprouts when offered; when it is absent, take Gravy Heart; when both are absent, fill an empty weapon slot; otherwise reroll. Keep writing the reroll. From 2:17 to 3:36 on the prior queen death, prose reroll still took Sour Cream, Sprouting Eyes, Starch Plating, GPU, and Fertilizer to 2/5. Leave leaked names out of the line.
- Hearty Helping, Hash Rush, Hot Start, and Starch Plating stay off want until Gravy Heart is owned and Overgrowth exists. Starch Plating before Overgrowth stalled sprouts at 5/8 twice (4/5 at 3:16, 3/5 at 3:36). Gravy Splash, Butter Pat, Chives, and Couch Potato wait until Gravy Heart is owned.
- Six passives can be full before 4:00. A missing Gravy Heart can never be taken after that. An owned Gravy Heart stacks in its slot up to 5/5; keep it in want. Gravy Heart at 5/5 raised max HP from about 111 to 183 on the 17:20 run. One level left the bar near 125, and fry-flies did 94 to it. This run still had the sixth slot open at 3:24 and Gravy Heart was never owned, so the bar stayed 111.
- After Overgrowth, take Hot Start once if it is missing, then name only Afterburner Fries until it is 8, then one chest for Jet Fryer. Any Hot Start before Overgrowth is a leaked slot. If Hot Start is still unowned by 5:00 and a weapon slot is open, name Small Fry until 8, then one chest for Big Fry. After Overgrowth and after Gravy Heart is owned, stack Gravy Heart, then Starch Plating, Hearty Helping, and Hash Rush.
- Gravy Heart outranks the other sustain cards. Stack it whenever it is owned, including when the weapon card is absent and when the other passive slots are full.
- Reroll Mash Finisher, Lean Lunch, Slow Cooker, Sour Cream, Sprouting Eyes, GPU, Packed Pan, Spry Sprout, Spud Sneakers, Rotten Spud, and Masher Grip. A second Fertilizer level is the same leak: the 7:18 row was Fertilizer 5/5 with no Gravy Heart.
- If the planned catalyst is absent and one other pair is already 8 with its catalyst maxed, open that one chest while the bar is full, health is steady, and the near ring is empty, then walk past every later chest.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still while any enemy is inside 60. A heading whose condition is already true is a standstill. Away-until-outside-80 gives no heading once the boss is outside 80.
- The line names one destination, then one level-up clause that stays correct after the named card is picked. The coach is read for a while, so the destination has to stay true when a circle appears before the next consult.
- During any boss minute the destination is this, in order: step sideways off any circle you are on; if hot oil is ticking, stay off it until the ticks stop; otherwise walk toward the thinner side that is not toward the boss. Copy that for the whole minute, including consults where no circle is showing and she is outside 50. Food is not a destination in that minute. The 3:04 line held health at 105/111 through her telegraph. The 3:21 line walked out of the pack toward food from 67/111, and the run was over at 3:24.
- Straight away from the boss is not that destination. The 3:36 line was only that, and her circle landed at 0.9s from 65 away. Fry-flies did 94, the queen 12. Thinner side, not toward her. On this run her body did 4 and the flies did 72. Marks also land from well outside melee.
- Outside a boss minute, and only the first that applies: off a circle; out of hot oil until the ticks stop; if about a third of the bar just dropped, away from the side that dealt it; away from a fry-fly pack while it is the damage; toward food only when enemies are inside 60, health is under two thirds, and the damage is not flies; away from the densest side. Empty direction bins are not a dense side and are not a stop.
- A clear near ring is not safety. Food does not outheal fry-flies (86% to 5% in 30 seconds on the 12:23 run; 95% to 67/111 then dead on this one). A fast drop names no chest and no shrine.
- A chest and a health fallback in one line send the bot to the chest. When health is falling, a boss is inside 80, a circle is out, or oil is ticking, the line has no chest.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss attacks are aimed at the tile. Step sideways out of the lane. Clause one stays the sidestep for the whole boss minute, including consults where no circle is showing yet.
- A boss inside 80 means do not close distance. It does not replace the sidestep or the thinner-side heading. Inside 50 is melee. The circle is still the threat when she is well outside melee, and the fly pack is the threat when her own hit is small.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a low bar. Step out before food or a chest, and do not turn back onto the oil. A second destination walks back onto the oil. Oil-drip was 13 on this run, behind the flies and the crumb-ants.
- On this Daily: Amusing around 2:00, Colorado Queen around 3:00, The Masher around 4:00, Late Blight around 6:00, Sir Strawman around 7:00, The Fryer King around 8:00 with oil, Great Wireworm around 10:00, The Mandoline around 12:00, Deep Freezer around 14:00, Grater Golem around 16:00, Compost Titan around 17:00. Queen plus fry-flies is the early wipe while sprouts are short of 8. Late Blight is passed once Overgrowth exists. On the longer runs the late chip was Great Wireworm, harvest mice, field rats, and fry-flies. Deep Freezer and Compost Titan are the late circles; mud golems and grease fiends finish a low bar.
- Circles first, then out of oil, then the thinner side not toward a boss inside 80, then away from the swarm that is dealing the damage. Falling health outranks a chest, a shrine, food, and a gap in the crowd.
