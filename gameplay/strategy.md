# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run from what happened. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

## Build

- Evolutions are the power spike: a weapon at level 8 plus its catalyst, then the next chest, opened while moving and off the densest tiles. The next weapon starts only after the current one is 8, and only when its catalyst is already owned.
- On this Daily that chain is Overgrowth, Jet Fryer, then Big Fry. Seeker Sprouts to 8 and Fertilizer (chest at 3:22), then Afterburner Fries to 8 with Hot Start already owned (Jet Fryer by 5:00), then Small Fry to 8 with Fertilizer already owned (Big Fry after 8:00). Until that weapon is 8, every level-up that offers it takes it.
- When that weapon is absent, take its missing catalyst. Otherwise take only sustain: Hearty Helping, Gravy Heart, Hash Rush, or Starch Plating. Those four stayed on the want list all run; only Starch Plating reached 5/5, and max HP stayed about 111.
- Hold every other weapon, and hold Couch Potato and Gravy Splash, until the current evolution exists. This run ended with Frozen Fries 8/8, Tot Toss 5/8, Hot Potato 8/8, Couch Potato 5/5, and Gravy Splash 5/5. Afterburner Fries and Small Fry took early levels; they get nothing more until their own turn.
- After Big Fry, one more pair: Hot Potato to 8 only if Hot Sauce is already owned, then Spicy Hot Potato from a chest that is already on the way out. Both pieces were finished by 10:02 and the chest was still unopened at 12:23, including one 143 away at 11:00. If that chest is far or health is falling, leave it. Three evolutions and a full bar beat a fourth evolution inside the swarm.
- Skip chests that evolve nothing, shrines, and any chest inside the swarm.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still with enemies close.
- Order is strict: off a tile where a boss attack will land, then out of hot oil or puddles, then out of the near-ring swarm toward food when health is under two thirds or just fell fast, then sideways off a boss lane, and only then away from the densest side.
- A telegraph under 1.5 seconds overrides the coach line, food, and the crowd. Leave any telegraph first. Never name a one-shot step that is still the plan after the circle is gone.
- A clear near ring is not safety: sprout mites have wiped a bar with 0 enemies within 60. A crowded near ring is the threat on its own: field rats took this run from 86% to 4% in 30 seconds with about 23 enemies inside 60. Falling health means leave that pack toward food, and the line drops the chest and the shrine.
- A fast drop (half the bar within about 30 seconds) means leave toward food now. Food does not outheal the burst by itself.
- The coach is read for a while. Write a line that is still correct a minute later. Once health is falling, the line is telegraph, oil, then food, with no chest on it.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss attacks are aimed at the tile. Step sideways out of the lane. Distance from the body does not dodge the circle: a Fryer King shot has landed from about 300, and Late Blight has killed from 76.
- A boss body inside 50 is a reason to step away. The circle is still the threat when the boss is well outside 50.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a low bar. Step out before food or a chest.
- On this Daily: Amusing around 2:00, The Masher around 4:00, Late Blight around 6:00, Sir Strawman around 7:00, The Fryer King around 8:00 with oil, Great Wireworm around 10:00, The Mandoline around 12:00 with a checker telegraph. Field rats led damage (294), then Great Wireworm (88), The Mandoline (56), Sir Strawman (51), and fry-flies. After 10:00, rat swarms are the health problem; circles are still the first step.
- Falling health outranks a chest, a shrine, and a gap in the crowd.
