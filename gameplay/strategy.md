# Strategy

The generic playbook for tibo.dog, rewritten after every run. Keep it short and specific; drop advice that runs disproved.

## Goal

Survive 20 minutes of the Daily on Nightmare.

That minute the line is the hazard underfoot, then the thinner side once the ground is clear. Put the evolution name in want on the consult that opens the chest. One ready name: Thousand Fries if Fry Knives and Salt Shaker are ready, otherwise Hungry Sprouts, otherwise Permafrost Fries if Frozen Fries and Thick Skin are ready, otherwise the other ready pair.

## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still while any enemy is inside 60. A heading whose condition is already true is a standstill. The line is read until the next consult, so it has to stay true after the circle expires.
- One destination, then one level-up clause that stays correct after the named card is picked. Write the first destination that applies. A second heading walks back onto the tile just left. Wide-run oil was 140 at 6:07 and 109 at 3:21 under an exit plus another heading. The 22:10 clear took 3 oil.
- On a circle, in hot oil, in a damaging puddle, or standing where a boss attack lands, the line is that exit and no other heading. Nearby, beside you, nothing underfoot, and the thinner side do not exit a landing timer. Already clear, walk the thinner side that is not toward a boss inside 80, and keep that heading for the rest of the minute. Two bosses up: the thinner side toward neither. Food and chests are not destinations in that minute. At 22:09 the line still joined the exit to the thinner side while the Mandoline timer ran from 0.8s to 0.0s and that boss closed to 53.
- Walk the thinner side, and do not close. Straight away from the boss is the wrong heading. The queen circle landed at 0.9s from 65 away. Inside 50 is melee. Circles, oil, and puddles stay the threat from outside melee.
- Outside a boss minute, the first that applies: off a circle or a landing attack; out of hot oil or a damaging puddle until the ticks stop, with no other heading; if about a third of the bar just dropped, the thinner side; toward food when enemies are inside 60, health is under two thirds, and the damage is not a swarm; away from the densest side. Empty direction bins are not a dense side and are not a stop.
- Field rats, harvest mice, grease fiends, ice imps, fry-flies, and sizzle-imps are never the destination. The 12:04 rat heading took 290 from field rats. The 22:10 thinner side listed none. Food does not outheal a swarm or a puddle. A fast drop names no chest and no shrine.
- A chest and a health fallback in one line send the bot to the chest. When health is falling, a boss is inside 80, a circle or a landing timer is up, or oil or a puddle is ticking, the line has no chest.

## Bosses and hazards

- Boss attacks are aimed at the tile. On a circle or a landing timer, the sidestep is the whole line. Already clear, the thinner side is the whole line, because the consult does not fire again when the telegraph ends.
- A boss inside 80 means do not close. It does not replace an exit or the thinner-side walk. On the 22:10 clear, Amusing dealt 2 and Compost Titan dealt 41.
- Hot oil and damaging puddles tick through a full bar. While oil is ticking, the line is the exit and nothing else. The 22:10 Fryer King stretch took 3 oil. The 12:04 Fryer King minute walked the thinner side and hot oil ended at 154.
- On this Daily: Amusing around 2:00, Colorado Queen around 3:00, The Masher around 4:00, Late Blight around 6:00, Sir Strawman around 7:00, The Fryer King around 8:00 with oil, Great Wireworm around 10:00, The Mandoline around 12:00, Deep Freezer around 14:00, Grater Golem around 16:00, Compost Titan around 17:00, Ketchup Kraken around 18:00 while Compost Titan is still alive, Harvester around 20:00 while those two are still up. The 22:10 thinner side crossed the 18:00 overlap on a 151 bar. Harvester dealt 390 and Kraken 322 across the whole run. Kraken hit for 64 from far at 20:49, and the bar climbed back from 10% by 21:02.
- On a hazard, exit it. Already clear, walk the thinner side away from a boss inside 80. Falling health outranks a chest, a shrine, food, and a gap in the crowd.
