## Movement

- Weapons fire on their own. Keep moving, and never stand still while any enemy is inside 60. A heading whose condition is already true is a standstill. The line is read until the next consult, so the heading has to stay true after the circle expires.
- One heading, then one level-up clause that stays correct after the named card is picked. While a circle, a landing timer, hot oil, or a damaging puddle is underfoot, the movement clause is one step off that ground and nothing else: no second direction, no already-clear clause, no then. Point the step at the thinner side, away from a boss inside 80. A second heading walks back onto the tile just left. That joined line killed the 22:10 clear on a Mandoline landing and killed the 26:14 record when Kraken closed from 33 to 21.
- Inside 50 the one heading is away from that boss, including while the landing is underfoot. Do not close does not leave melee. Outside 50, straight away from the boss is the wrong heading. Two bosses, neither inside 50: the thinner side toward neither. That walk crossed the 18:00 overlap on a full 164 bar.
- Already clear, walk the thinner side and do not close on a boss inside 80. Keep that heading for the rest of a boss minute. Food and chests are not destinations in that minute. The queen circle landed at 0.9s from 65 away.
- Outside a boss minute, the first that applies: one step off a circle, a landing, hot oil, or a puddle until the ticks stop; if about a third of the bar just dropped, the thinner side; toward food when enemies are inside 60, health is under two thirds, and the damage is not a swarm; away from the densest side. Empty direction bins are not a dense side and are not a stop.
- Field rats, harvest mice, grease fiends, ice imps, fry-flies, and sizzle-imps are never the destination. The 26:14 ledger has no field rats. The 12:04 rat heading took 290. Food does not outheal a swarm or a puddle. A fast drop names no chest and no shrine.
- A chest and a health fallback in one line send the bot to the chest. When health is falling, a boss is inside 80, a circle or a landing timer is up, or oil or a puddle is ticking, the line has no chest.
- A sticky patch slows the step. While it is underfoot with a landing timer, the one heading leaves the patch and the timer and goes away from a boss inside 50.
