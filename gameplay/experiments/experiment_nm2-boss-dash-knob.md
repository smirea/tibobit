## Hypothesis

bossDashThreshold 0.25 with bossDashRange 150 (and dashThreshold 0.5) makes the bot dash when the Mascot is close, instead of spending 7 of 8 dashes on pests and firing only when every move is lethal.

## Method

Fresh live NM2 seeds, at least 5 per arm, paired with the unchanged knobs. Compare dashes and their targets, Mascot damage, hp at 2:30 and survival.

## Findings

Prompted by L4.I10: dash prompt 19-47% from 2:12 to 2:25, dashed at 51% when all moves were dead (B92, B118).
