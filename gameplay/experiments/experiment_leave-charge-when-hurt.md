## Hypothesis

In 3 of 4 recent full runs 80-100 hp was lost in under 11 s in the 1:04-1:56 window while a goal held Tibo in place. A planner.goal text that rates keep-away highest whenever hurt_by shows damage in the last 3 s, and charge, open and fight goals only when hurt_by is empty, cuts bursts and health lost 1:00-2:30.

## Method

Seeker-sprouts, playbook knobs, 240 s on s31-40 against the playbook games already on file. Manipulation check: charge or open goals end within 3 s of damage. Metrics: bursts of 40 hp or more in 5 s, health lost 1:00-2:30, chests listed, survival to 4:00. If the infra knob for aborting a charge on damage exists, add it as a second arm. Adopt only on 8 or more of 10 alive and 3 or more net paired wins, or a large drop in burst count with no loss of chests.
