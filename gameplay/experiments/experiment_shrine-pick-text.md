## Hypothesis

The default picked Gold gain twice and Move speed in the full run and left gold unspent. A planner.pick text for shrines that prefers Armor, then Max HP, then Damage, then Cooldown or Move speed, and never Gold gain, Luck, Pickup range or XP gain, raises armor and max HP at 2:00 and cuts damage taken by 3:00.

Seeker-sprouts, playbook knobs, 240 s on s31-40, shrine pick text only. Metrics: shrines used, armor and max HP at 2:00, health lost by 3:00, survival to 4:00. Paired against the playbook games on file.
