## Hypothesis

Starch Plating 5 in place of Stuffed Skin cuts the damage of each hit, which blunts the many small ice-imp fan and pest hits and the boss bursts, and lengthens life on the lo-surv gym.

## Method

Two games from nm1-min16-lo-surv with the passive loadout swapped (Starch Plating replaces Stuffed Skin), playbook unchanged; compare with the lo-surv baselines (18:07, 19:13, plus this iteration's repeat) on time of death, damage by source and minimum hp.

## Findings

L2.I3: Starch Plating 5 for Stuffed Skin died at 19:40 and 20:18, better than the hp/dash baselines (17:53-19:13) but not better than damage passives (20:07 twice). Not adopted.
