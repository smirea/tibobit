## Hypothesis

Fry-lance, gravy-boat, spud-lob, deep-fryer and seeker-sprouts deal several times Steam Vent's damage by 2:00, so they kill early elites, open chests, evolve earlier and reach the 2:00 Mascot with more dps and hp.

## Method

Live fresh NM2 games with the playbook unchanged except the start weapon, 300s, paired on the same fresh seeds (never TIBO10) against steam-vent. Record damage dealt by 2:00, elites killed by 2:00, chests opened, hp at 2:00, first evolution, Mascot damage and survival. Judge on those continuous metrics as well as on survival.
