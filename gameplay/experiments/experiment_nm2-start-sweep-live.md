# Do untried start weapons deal enough early damage on live NM2 to get elites, chests and past the Mascot

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | done |
| Area | build |
| Result | confirmed |
| Created | L8.I10 |
| Updated | L9.I10 |

## Hypothesis

Fry-lance, gravy-boat, spud-lob, deep-fryer and seeker-sprouts deal several times Steam Vent's damage by 2:00, so they kill early elites, open chests, evolve earlier and reach the 2:00 Mascot with more dps and hp.

## Method

Live fresh NM2 games, playbook unchanged except the start weapon, 300 s. L9.I1 plays all five on the same fresh seed 6161. L9.I2 adds Steam Vent controls and repeats the best starts on a second fresh seed, never TIBO10. Record damage dealt by 2:00, elites killed by 2:00, chests opened, hp at 2:00, first evolution, amusing damage and survival. A start needs a clearly higher damage-by-2:00 and chest flow on at least 5 paired seeds to enter the playbook.

## Findings

Confirmed on the damage-and-chest criterion only: deep-fryer beat Steam Vent on damage by 2:00 in 6 of 6 pairs and on chests in 4 of 6. The L9.I10 rehearsal showed deep-fryer lived 0 of 5 and died sooner than the old playbook (B171), so the survival gain stays unproven. Frozen-fries, spud-cannon, seeker-sprouts, fry-lance, gravy-boat and spud-lob did not beat it.
