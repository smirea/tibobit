## Hypothesis

With the chest-first playbook, a peeler start (evolves to the healing Mandoline Fury with Gravy Heart) survives 5:00 on a new seed with at most the damage of a steam-vent start, and reaches an evolution as early.

## Method

Two fresh NM1 games on seed 909, 300s, differing only in starting weapon: steam-vent and peeler. Compare damage by source, boss chests, evolutions and their times, and level. Both 5:00 states are saved as gyms.

## Findings

I8: steam-vent took 60 damage with 19 boss chests and 3 evolutions; peeler took 218 damage with 14 chests and 2 evolutions. Steam-vent stays the starting weapon. One seed and one game each.
