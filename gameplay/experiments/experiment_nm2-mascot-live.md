# Does a boss-adjacent evade rule and a boss-slam dash prompt get a live NM2 build through the 2:00 Festival Mascot

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | done |
| Area | bosses |
| Result | refuted |
| Created | sonnet-high L3.I10 |
| Updated | L4.I3 |

## Hypothesis

A goal text rating Evade highest when a boss is within 120 units (any hp), no low-hp elite fights before 2:30, and a boss-only dash prompt cut Mascot damage and early deaths compared with the playbook text.

## Method

L4.I1: the package on live fresh NM2 steam-vent seeds 1919, 2020, 4141 and 4242 (300s), plus an unchanged-playbook control on 4141. Reference is 1 of 6 alive at 5:00 live. The package needs 4 of 4 alive at 5:00, first chest by about 2:30 and an evolution by 4:00 to count as beating it. Compare boss:amusing damage, dashes spent on bosses vs pests, chests by 4:00, hp at 2:30 and survival at 5:00. Next: split into evade text and dash prompt, extend to at least 5 seeds per arm, and replay the pre-mascot gyms.

## Findings

L4.I1/L4.I2: the package lived 2 of 4 live (2020 and 4242 alive; 1919 died 2:35, 4141 died 1:50). The unchanged control lived 2 of 4 on the same seeds (2020 and 4242 alive; 1919 died 2:30, 4141 died 3:06). The package took more damage on the survivor seeds. No benefit over the control. Deaths are minute-1 pest chip plus missing early power (B95, B97), not Mascot handling.
