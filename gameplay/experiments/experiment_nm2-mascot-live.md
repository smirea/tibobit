## Hypothesis

A goal text rating Evade highest when a boss is within about 120 units (any hp), a dash prompt that says yes on a boss telegraph or hit, and no elite fights below 70% hp between 1:30 and 2:00 cut Mascot damage and early deaths compared with the playbook text.

## Method

From a live-built pre-mascot gym (about 1:50, nm2-live-pre-mascot) and fresh live NM2 seeds, 300s: playbook baseline vs evade exception vs dash prompt vs both, at least 5 paired seeds per arm. Compare boss:amusing damage, dashes used on the boss, chests by 4:00, survival at 5:00.

## Findings

L4.I1: the full package (goal text with a 120-unit boss evade exception at any hp and no low-hp elite fights from 1:30 to 2:30, plus a narrow boss-hit dash prompt) plays 5 fresh live NM2 seeds (1919, 2020, 4141, 4242, 4343). Baseline reference is 1 of 6 alive at 5:00 live. At least 4 of 5 alive counts as beating it. Then split the package into evade text and dash prompt, and watch for chest starvation on 1919 and 2020.
