# Do the boss dash knobs lift live NM2 5:00 survival on seeds never used before

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | done |
| Area | bosses |
| Result | refuted |
| Created | L8.I9 |
| Updated | L8.I10 |

## Hypothesis

bossDashThreshold 0.25 with bossDashRange 150 keeps dash charges for the Mascot and the 3:00 and 4:00 bosses. They lived 4 of 10 across two arms on seeds that kill the playbook, against a pooled baseline of about 5 of 21. Fresh seeds remove the selection on dying seeds.

## Method

Three live fresh NM2 steam-vent games of 300s on seeds 5353, 5454 and 5555 with only the boss knobs changed. The eliteFrom and explore games on 5353 and 5555 share the knobs and add two more samples as secondary evidence. Compare alive at 5:00, first evolution, chests, dashes spent near bosses, and hp at 2:00. Counting all five games, 4 of 5 alive with an evolution by about 2:30 is the confirmation bar; 2 or fewer of 5 means refuted. Unpaired against the pooled baseline, so a pass needs a paired rehearsal before shipping.

## Findings

Boss dash knobs on fresh seeds 5353, 5454 and 5555 lived 0 of 3 (died 2:04, 2:09, 3:44), 0 of 5 with the two side games. The earlier 2 of 5 was selection on dying seeds. See B148.
