## Hypothesis

Explore goals end at 300 units or the first new chest. A longer exploreDistance (600-800) reaches new chests and spots sooner and with fewer re-plans, which raises chests opened by 2:00 on seeds where the first chest is listed after 1:20.

## Method

Seeker-sprouts with playbook knobs on seeds 31-34, 300 s, exploreDistance 700 against 300 on the same seeds. Read the first chest listed and opened, chests opened by 2:00, damage taken in the opening, and 5:00 survival. Games next iteration.
