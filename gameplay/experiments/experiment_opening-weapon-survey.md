## Hypothesis

Base weapons differ a lot in damage, kills and survival over the first 3 minutes of Nightmare 1. Piercing and aura weapons may carry the opening best, and a passive bundle (max HP, cooldown, move speed, area) should raise a weak starter's damage and survival.

## Method

Fresh Nightmare 1 games on seed 11 for 180 s, each starting with a different base weapon alone: fry-knives, hash-boomerang, tater-mines, mezzaluna. A second fry-knives game adds gravy-heart, chives, spud-sneakers and big-bowl at level 2. Compare damage by weapon, kills, health trend and level reached.
