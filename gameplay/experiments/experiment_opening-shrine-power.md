## Hypothesis

The planner rarely charges Spud Shrines in the opening, so builds stay at 120 max hp and 0 armor. A planner.goal prompt that rates Spud Shrines within 300 units above exploring while health is above 60% and no elite is near gives armor and max HP by 2:00 and cuts opening damage.

## Method

Seeker-sprouts with the playbook knobs, 240 s from 0:00 on seeds 31-40, shrine goal prompt against the playbook on the same seeds. Read shrines charged, max HP and armor at 2:00, damage taken by 2:30, survival to 3:00 and 5:00. Keep the stat pick text the default.
