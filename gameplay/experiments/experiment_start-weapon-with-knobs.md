## Hypothesis

Seeker-sprouts depends on Fertilizer, which the planner rejects. A start whose catalyst is attractive (spud-lob, starch-bolt or spud-cannon) levels and evolves sooner, so it lives 5:00 on more fresh seeds than seeker-sprouts with the knobs on.

## Method

Fresh seeds 31-35 from 0:00, 300 s, playbook knobs on, one arm per start weapon (spud-lob, starch-bolt, spud-cannon) against seeker-sprouts on the same seeds. Read 5:00 survival, chests by 2:00, evolution time, weapon levels at 5:00.
