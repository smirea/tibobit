## Hypothesis

The playbook reaches 5:00 on about half of fresh seeds. A pooled baseline over 20 seeds fixes the noise floor so later arms can be judged against it.

## Method

Run the playbook on seeds 36-45 alongside the paired arms of the other experiments, and pool with seeds 31-35 and the rehearsals. Report 5:00 survival, deaths by time window and killer.
