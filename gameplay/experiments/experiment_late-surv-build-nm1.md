# Does an hp and dash passive build survive 16:40-21:40 on Nightmare 1 better than the damage passive build

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | done |
| Area | build |
| Result | refuted |
| Coach | sonnet-high |
| Created | sonnet-high L2.I1 |
| Updated | sonnet-high L2.I4 |

## Hypothesis

With the same weapons, Butter Socks (3 dash charges) and Stuffed Skin in place of Butter Pat and Aged Russet give less damage taken and a longer life through the 17:00 Compost Titan and 18:00 Ketchup Kraken. This would show that level-up and chest picks should favour dashes and hp over a sixth damage passive.

## Method

From the 16:40 gyms nm1-min16-lo-full and nm1-min16-lo-surv (same weapons at level 8, two passives differ), play 300s with the unchanged playbook. Run lo-full twice for variance. Compare damage by source, minimum hp, dashes used and survival past 20:00.

## Findings

L2.I3 swapped the damage passives into the lo-surv map: both games passed 20:00 (died 20:07 to the Harvester) against hp/dash baselines of 18:07, 19:13 and 17:53. Lo-full with damage passives passed 20:00 twice. The hp/dash build is worse; the map is not the cause. Pick policy should not trade damage passives for hp or dash.
