## Hypothesis

The live opening dies to reaction lag against chargers and the 2:00 Mascot. Live-tested knob or prompt changes (distrustSeconds, extraDamage 0, a pick prompt that levels steam-vent first, a dash prompt for the Mascot) may raise the 5 of 5 lockstep survival back toward the live 1 of 5 baseline.

## Method

Fresh NM2 steam-vent seeds, 300s, live, 3 or more seeds per variant against the unchanged playbook: measure deaths before 5:00, damage by source, first chest, first evolution and dashes.

## Findings

Live baseline 1 of 6 alive at 5:00 (L3.I10 rehearsal 1 of 5, full run died 2:15 to the Mascot). L3.I9 games appear live: baselines 2 of 2 alive, catalyst picks 1 of 3. L4.I1 puts the Mascot package on 5 fresh seeds. L4.I2 onward: commit-the-weapon picks, routing and starting weapon, each paired live on at least 5 seeds.
