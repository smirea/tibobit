## Hypothesis

Three handed items (baked-buckler, spud-muffin, porcupine-spud) lived 4:00 on 5 of 5 seeds against 3 of 5 for the playbook, and armor-first picks without the porcupine item lived 2 of 8. Porcupine-spud plus Starch Plating (Fry Volcano) is therefore the likelier engine, so one handed porcupine-spud should live 4:00 on more seeds than one handed baked-buckler.

## Method

Seeker-sprouts with the playbook knobs, 240 s from 0:00 on s31-35. Arm A hands porcupine-spud x1; arm B hands baked-buckler x1 (armor 10) as a control for any single item. Paired with the playbook games on file (5:00, 5:00, 3:05, 5:00, 2:39). Metrics chosen first: alive at 4:00, damage taken, Starch Plating level and armor at 2:00. Manipulation check: Starch Plating offered and taken in arm A, and Fry Volcano evolved. Items are handed at 0:00, so this is an upper bound and a lead only. If arm A wins, the next step is a route to get porcupine-spud from play (crate, Fry Cart, Microwave).
