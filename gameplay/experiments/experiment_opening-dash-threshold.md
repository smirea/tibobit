## Hypothesis

Dashes fire only under about 35% health at dashThreshold 0.5. In the full run 12 asks scored 17-43% during a 79-health burst. dashThreshold 0.3 with bossDashThreshold 0.3 and bossDashRange 250 dashes earlier, so opening bursts and the 2:00 amusing boss cost less and more games reach 3:00 and 5:00.

## Method

Seeker-sprouts with the playbook knobs, 240 s games from 0:00 on fresh seeds 31-40, dash arm against the playbook on the same seeds (reuse s31-35 playbook games, play s36-40 for both arms). Read burst size (health lost in 5 s), dash count and health at each dash, health at 2:30, survival to 3:00 and 5:00. A change needs a clear gain over at least 8 paired seeds.
