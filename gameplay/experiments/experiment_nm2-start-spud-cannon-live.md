# Does a spud-cannon start beat deep-fryer on early damage, chests and hp at 2:00 on live NM2

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | done |
| Area | build |
| Result | refuted |
| Created | L9.I8 |
| Updated | L9.I9 |

## Hypothesis

Spud-cannon is a targeted explosive shot that clears pest packs in minute 0-1 and may give more early damage and elite kills than deep-fryer, which lifts hp at 2:00 and chest flow.

## Method

5 live fresh NM2 games of 300s on seeds 6161, 6262, 6363, 6464 and 6565, paired with the deep-fryer and steam-vent games on the same seeds. Record damage by 2:00, elites, chests, first chest time, minute-1 hp loss, hp at 2:00 and survival. A replacement needs clearly higher damage and chests on at least 5 pairs.

## Findings

Lived 2 of 5 paired fresh seeds (s6262, s6363 alive; s6161, s6464, s6565 died 2:27, 3:06, 2:08). Damage by 2:00 was 7.4k-10.2k, not clearly above deep-fryer on the same seeds, and chests by 2:00 were 1, 5, 2, 0 and 3. Both survivors had an early chest (0:42 and 1:10); the dead games lost 59-83 hp in minute 1. It does not replace deep-fryer. See B169.
