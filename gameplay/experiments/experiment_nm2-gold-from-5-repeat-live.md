## Hypothesis

A goal rule that rates Potato Crate, Fry Cart, Whetstone and Spud Shrine like a chest from about 300 gold with no boss within 300 units converts the 1500-2000 gold held by 5:00-7:00 into items and stats, so hp, crates and time lived from the 5:00 gyms rise against the plain playbook.

## Method

Live gyms nm2-live-min5 and nm2-live-min5-fryer, 300 s each, 3-4 games per arm (plain playbook vs spend-from-5:00). Record crates, shrines and whetstone levels, gold unspent at death, hp at 6:00 and 7:00, damage by source and time lived. Beat the plain arm on time lived or hp with no more damage in at least 3 of 4 pairs.
