# What is the live survival rate of the unchanged playbook on fresh Nightmare 2 seeds

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | done |
| Area | meta |
| Result | confirmed |
| Created | L5.I2 |
| Updated | L8.I10 |

## Hypothesis

The unchanged live playbook (steam-vent, chest-first goal with the 10:00 gold rule, default picks) lives 5:00 on about 1 of 4 fresh NM2 seeds. A new seed extends that baseline and checks that the seeds picked for tests are not unusually hard.

## Method

Unchanged playbook, live, fresh NM2 seeds never used before, 300s each, one per iteration. Compare survival, chests, first evolution and hp at 2:00 with the pooled baseline of about 5 of 19.

## Findings

The unchanged playbook lived 0 of 2 on seeds 5151 and 5252 and 0 of 5 in the L8.I10 rehearsal, putting the live rate at about 5 of 26 (about 20%). The baseline is confirmed low. See B112 and B150.
