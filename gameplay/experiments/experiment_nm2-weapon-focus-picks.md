## Hypothesis

A pick prompt that levels the strongest weapon held (and takes its catalyst when it reaches max-1), allows at most 3 weapons and bans on-dash weapons, gives levels above 3 and an earlier evolution than default picks, and lowers early damage taken.

## Method

Fresh live NM2 seeds, at least 5 per arm, paired with default picks. Compare weapon levels at 2:00 and 4:00, first evolution, dps proxy (damage dealt), damage taken and survival.

## Findings

Prompted by L4.I10: six weapons at 1/8 by 1:42, no passive (B119). The earlier commit test (L4.I3) used Steam Vent only and died 0 of 2.
