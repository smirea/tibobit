# Does a Starch Bolt start (aim-free random strikes) carry the live NM2 seeds that kill Steam Vent

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | done |
| Area | build |
| Result | refuted |
| Created | L8.I8 |
| Updated | L8.I9 |

## Hypothesis

Starch Bolt strikes random pests within 160 units with no aiming, giving more early damage than Steam Vent (about 4000 dealt by 2:15). That should bring elite kills, chests and an early evolution on the seeds where the unchanged playbook lived 0 of 5.

## Method

Four fresh live NM2 games of 300s with starch-bolt and the unchanged playbook on 1919, 4141, 2121 and 4444, where the Steam Vent controls died (2:30/2:35, 3:06/1:50, 2:27, 2:15). Compare alive at 5:00, hp at 1:00 and 2:00, damage dealt by 2:15, chests, first evolution and elites killed. Four seeds is a lead only; confirmation needs 4 of 5 alive with an evolution by about 2:30 on five seeds.

## Findings

L8.I8: Starch Bolt lived 0 of 4 on 1919, 4141, 2121 and 4444 (died 2:11, 2:20, 1:53, 3:10), no evolution, 0-4 chests, same as the Steam Vent controls. See B146.
