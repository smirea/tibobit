## Hypothesis

Raising eliteSeconds from 20 to 35 lets Tibo finish elites it currently drops, so more chests drop and evolutions come sooner, without raising damage taken.

## Method

From nm1-min5-fresh, 300s, chest-first playbook with eliteSeconds 35. Compare elites killed, boss chests, evolutions and damage with the earlier chest-first games on this gym (I5.G3, I6.G2, I6.G3, I7.G5, I9.G2, I9.G3).

## Findings

I8: eliteSeconds 35 survived but took 266 damage, had 12 boss chests, 2 evolutions at 7:52 and 8:53, and dropped 7 elite goals (5 done, 2 gave up). No clear gain over eliteSeconds 20; kept at 20.
