## Hypothesis

Tot-toss is an aim-free targeted shot (Tot Gatling with Chives) and may give early damage, elite kills and chests comparable to or above deep-fryer.

## Method

Record damage by 2:00, elites, chests, first chest time, minute-1 hp loss, hp at 2:00 and survival. A replacement needs clearly higher damage and chest flow on at least 5 pairs.
