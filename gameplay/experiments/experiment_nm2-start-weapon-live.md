# Does a stronger starting weapon (peeler, root-spikes) carry the live NM2 seeds that kill Steam Vent past 5:00

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | done |
| Area | build |
| Result | inconclusive |
| Created | L4.I4 |
| Updated | L4.I7 |

## Hypothesis

Steam Vent's early damage is too low to kill elites and produce chests. Peeler (Mandoline Fury heals) or Root Spikes (3 strikes per 2.4 s) reaches elites, chests and an evolution by about 2:00 and lives 5:00 on seeds 1919 and 4141, where Steam Vent died at 2:30 and 3:06.

## Method

Fresh live NM2, seeds 1919 and 4141, 300 s, unchanged playbook and default picks, one weapon per arm. Compare first chest, first evolution, elites killed, chests by 4:00, damage dealt, damage taken and survival against the Steam Vent controls. If an arm lives both, extend it to 2020, 4242 and 3 new seeds against Steam Vent.

## Findings

L4.I4: peeler lived 1 of 2 (4141 alive with Mandoline Fury 1:30 and 18 chests; 1919 died 2:03). Root Spikes died 0 of 2. Steam Vent controls lived 0 of 2 on those seeds, but across all live seeds it lives about 3 of 10. No startable weapon has reliably carried the dying seeds. Steam Vent stays the start.
