## Hypothesis

A planner.pick template that tells the planner to take the catalyst for any weapon at level 6 or higher (and recipe parts that complete an evolution) before new weapons makes evolutions land earlier than the default pick text.

## Method

Same start (seeker-sprouts) on at least 3 fresh seeds from 0:00, 300 s each, default pick prompt against the catalyst-aware pick prompt. Compare evolution time, chests opened by 2:30, health at 3:00 and death time.
