## Hypothesis

Chests only drop from elites and bosses. In the full run the first elites were killed at about 1:52 and the first chest was listed at 1:54, later than usual. A planner.goal text that rates fight-the-elite high while health is 80% or more, no boss is near and the time is before 1:40 brings the first chest and level jumps earlier.

## Method

Seeker-sprouts, playbook knobs, 240 s on s31-40, goal text only, eliteSeconds unchanged. Metrics: first chest opened (use run.bossChests too), chests by 2:00, health lost 1:00-2:00, bursts of 40 hp or more in 5 s. Note B74 and B68: elite fights also burst, and withholding them before 2:00 did worse. Compare with the playbook games on file.
