# Does deep-fryer with Starch Plating 3 from 0:00 survive the live NM2 opening

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | done |
| Area | items |
| Result | refuted |
| Created | L9.I5 |
| Updated | L9.I7 |

## Hypothesis

Pest chip hits of 3-7 are cut hard by armor, and deep-fryer's extra dps and chests supply the evolution that armor alone lacked (Starch Plating 2 alone died at 3:58 and 4:04), so the combination lives past 5:00 on seeds where plain deep-fryer died.

## Method

Live fresh NM2 deep-fryer with a loadout of Starch Plating 3 and full hp on s6363, s6565 and s6161, 300 s, compared with the plain deep-fryer games on the same seeds (died 2:03, 2:18, 2:50). Record minute 0-1 hp loss, hp at 2:00, chests, first evolution and survival. An upper bound: a pick rule for armor is built only if this lives 2 or more of 3.

## Findings

The loadout lived 2 of 5 (B166), below the 4-of-5 bar. Armor alone does not carry the deep-fryer opening. Closed.
