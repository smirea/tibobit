# Does a defense-first passive pick text after the first evolution raise max HP and armor by 5:00 on fresh seeds from 0:00?

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | done |
| Area | build |
| Result | inconclusive |
| Created | L7.I1 |
| Updated | L7.I10 |

## Hypothesis

After the first evolution the default planner.pick spends passive picks on utility passives (Peel Out, Slow Cooker, Big Bowl), so max HP and armor stay near 134 and 12. A pick text that takes Gravy Heart, Starch Plating, Stuffed Skin or Root Network (and shrine Armor or Max HP) before a utility passive or any passive already at level 3 or more, and only once an evolved weapon is held, raises max HP and armor at 4:00 and 5:00 and lowers health lost 3:00-5:00 without cutting damage much.

## Method

Fresh seeker-sprouts on Nightmare 1 from 0:00, 300 s, seeds 41-44, both arms played now (unchanged playbook and the playbook with the defense-passives planner.pick). Metrics chosen first: max HP and armor at 4:00 and 5:00 (mean and spread), health lost 3:00-5:00, alive at 5:00. Manipulation check: picks lines after the first evolution show those passives taken over utility ones. Games with no evolution by 4:00 are counted separately. Four pairs are a lead for max HP and armor only; survival is not expected to move. Any adoption needs 10 paired seeds.

## Findings

No game started in L7.I1, I5, I6, I7, I8 or I9 (all 8 games per iteration failed at start). Nothing was learned about defense-first passive picks. The question stays a lead from the 10:14 run (B125), to be re-proposed when games start.
