# Does charging a Beetle Nest early at high health and then opening the dropped chest bring the first chest sooner and raise 4:00 survival?

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | done |
| Area | spots |
| Result | inconclusive |
| Created | L7.I3 |
| Updated | L7.I10 |

## Hypothesis

A Beetle Nest calls an elite pair that drops chests when killed. B109 (s40: nest at 0:27, chest at 0:52, Kettle Cyclone at 1:27, alive at 4:00) points one way and B101 (elite pairs arriving next to Tibo in nest games) the other. Charging the nest only at 80% health or more with no elite alive before 1:30, and then opening the dropped chest at once, should bring the first chest 30-60 s sooner than the playbook's 1:05-1:54. That should raise weapon levels by 2:00 and survival at 4:00.

## Method

Fresh seeker-sprouts from 0:00 on s31-40, 240 s, playbook knobs, only planner.goal changed: nest rated about 75% before 1:30 at 80% health or more with no elite on screen, dropped chest about 70%, below 50% health keep-away or food. Paired with the 10 playbook games on file (6 of 10 alive at 4:00). Metrics chosen first: chests opened by 2:00 (run.bossChests), alive at 4:00, health lost in minute 1, weapon levels at 2:00. Manipulation check: a Beetle Nest goal picked before 1:30 at 80% health or more; the 'Goals for spots that call something to Tibo' line shows elites killed and chests listed within 60 s; the chest is then opened. Games where no nest is on the map or picked count as no test. Adopt only on 8 or more of 10 alive with a net of +3 paired wins, then confirm on fresh seeds.

## Findings

All 20 games in L7.I3 and L7.I4 failed at start. The disagreement between B101 (nest brings an elite pair next to Tibo) and B109 (nest at 0:27 led to a chest at 0:52 on s40) stays unresolved.
