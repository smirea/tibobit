## Hypothesis

Goals that hold Tibo in place (charge a spot, open a chest, keep an elite in reach) are not ended in time by stuckSeconds or planEvery 5. holdAbortHurt 0.15 ends them when 18 hp is lost in 3 s, so the 40+ hp bursts at 1:04-1:56 and at the 2:00 boss shrink. Unlike the keep-away text with planEvery 2 (B98, B99), it does not drop chest goals when nothing hurts Tibo.

## Method

Seeker-sprouts, playbook knobs plus holdAbortHurt 0.15 only, default prompts, 240 s from 0:00 on s31-40, paired with the 10 playbook games on file (6 of 10 alive at 4:00). Manipulation check: spot, chest or elite goals logged as gave up or dropped within 3 s of hurt_by showing 15% of max HP or more. Metrics chosen first: bursts of 40 hp or more in 5 s, health lost 1:00-2:30, chests opened (checked against run.bossChests), alive at 4:00. Adopt only on 8 or more of 10 alive with 3 or more net paired wins.

## Findings

L5.I3 s31-40: arm 8 of 10 vs playbook 6 of 10. L5.I4 s41-45: 4 of 5 vs 4 of 5. L5.I5 s46-50: arm 2 of 5 vs playbook 4 of 5 (0 wins, 2 losses, 3 ties). Pooled s31-50: 14 of 20 vs 14 of 20, 5 wins, 5 losses, 10 ties, net 0. Manipulation check met in 3 of 5 L5.I5 arm games (goals left after 19-51 hp lost in 3 s) but survival was unchanged. The earlier lead was regression to the mean. Not added to the playbook; holdAbortHurt stays 0.
