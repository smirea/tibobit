# Experiments

High-level strategies to try on experiment runs, so the serious strategy does not settle into a local maximum. The coach picks one per experiment run, plays it on purpose even where strategy.md disagrees, and rewrites this list after every run: new ideas go in, results and reasons go under each entry, and ideas that are settled get retired. Results of each experiment run are logged in experiments.csv.

## Ideas

- **Two-boss edge** (untried): from Compost Titan's spawn, walk to the map edge away from the titan and keep moving along that edge once Ketchup Kraken spawns. Tests whether the thinner side still leaves you in the overlap that did titan 144 and grease fiends 105.
  No run yet. The 18:27 death was that overlap on a full 111 bar, with both bosses alive at 18:24 and the line a circle sidestep. Disagrees with the thinner-side rule the strategy now uses once the ground is clear.

- **Never heart** (untried): Gravy Heart is never in want. After Overgrowth, Starch Plating to 5, then Spry Sprout, and Salt Shaker is taken for Thousand Fries. Tests whether the record passive row repeats when it is the plan, and whether refusing the heart costs the 18:24 burst.
  No run yet. The serious 18:27 run landed that row by lockout, not by plan: Spud Sneakers, Fertilizer 4/5, Sprouting Eyes, Starch Plating 5/5, Spry Sprout 5/5, Salt Shaker, and a 111 bar outlived the 17:20 heart build. The strategy still takes a heart offered before the sixth passive. The last drop was about 99% to 16/111, so a 183 bar is the open counterfactual.

- **Thousand Fries first** (untried): after Overgrowth, want is only Fry Knives and Salt Shaker until that chest opens, then Hungry Sprouts. Other weapons wait. Tests whether Thousand Fries earlier than 16:28 beats spreading levels across Tailgate Fries, Frozen Fries, Locked Gatling, and Tot Toss.
  No run yet. The record had both evolutions and those four weapons at 8, with Thousand Fries only confirmed in at 16:28, and the build was finished before the death. Disagrees with leveling the whole record set together.

- **Greedy** (untried): take every chest, shrine and gold pickup in reach and the highest-damage card every level. Tests whether the safety-first play is leaving power on the table.
  No run yet. The 18:27 run left chests closed through boss minutes and still finished Thousand Fries and Hungry Sprouts with every weapon maxed by 17:30. The death was a standstill in the titan overlap, not a missing pickup. The 15:22 run also held chests and died earlier, with Hungry Sprouts never opened. Closed chests remain open as a variable; this record says the last minute was not one.

- **Safe** (untried): never walk toward anything worth having while any enemy is inside 100; take every health and armor card. Tests how far pure survival carries a weak build.
  No run yet. The 18:27 death was a standstill, not a pickup chase: full bar until 18:24, bosses kept past 100, ledger led by Compost Titan and grease fiends. Taking every health card still risks a passive row with no Starch Plating and no Salt Shaker. The early wide deaths were oil plus a second heading, which this package does not isolate.

- **Short-term focus** (untried): every pick is whatever kills the most enemies right now (new weapons first, then weapon levels), no recipe planning. Tests whether early kill speed beats evolution plans.
  No run yet. The record reached level 74 and 81209 kills and died in the two-boss overlap. The 15:22 run reached 36822 kills and died on a 111 bar to rats. Kill count past the early game keeps failing as an account of the long deaths.

- **Long-term focus** (untried): commit to two evolutions from the first level-up, reroll everything else, and open every chest the moment a pair is ready. Tests whether a strict plan reaches evolutions earlier than a flexible one.
  No run yet. The record was the flexible version and is the long run: Overgrowth at 5:00, Fertilizer leaked to 4/5, heart missed, Thousand Fries and Hungry Sprouts in late, plus the fries and gatling weapons. A strict reroll would have refused Salt Shaker, Starch Plating, and Spry Sprout, which is the row that just won. Still the untested half.

- **XP vacuum** (untried): prioritize xp gems and pickup-radius cards (Potato Magnet) over everything but safety. Tests whether level count is the bottleneck.
  No run yet. The record hit level 74 and two evolutions without hunting gems, and died on HP in the titan overlap. The 17:20 run ended at level 68 with the heart. Level count is not what separated 18:27 from a clear.

- **Evolve-then-wide** (untried): open Overgrowth before any extra weapon, then fill every remaining slot before Jet Fryer or Big Fry. Tests whether the extra weapons on the long runs extend a run only after the first evolution.
  No run yet. The record had three extra weapons by 2:00, Overgrowth at 5:00, and then the wide set, without rushing Jet Fryer. It is not a clean fill-after-evolution run. The pure delay of every other weapon until Overgrowth is still open, and it has to allow Thousand Fries and the record weapons or it refuses the long build.

- **First ready pair** (untried): evolve whichever weapon first reaches 8 with its catalyst, and open that chest on the beat, even when the weapon is not Seeker Sprouts. Tests whether any evolution before Late Blight passes that minute.
  No run yet. The record's first evolution was still Overgrowth, at 5:00. At 10:03 Big Fry and Thousand Fries were both ready and the chest stayed closed for a circle; Thousand Fries was in by 16:28. The deliberate non-sprout first pair is still open.

- **Oil-exit minute** (untried): play the normal sprout plan. After the first hot-oil or puddle tick in a boss minute, every later line until the next minute names only stepping off that ground, with no thinner-side clause. Tests whether the wide deaths were the compound heading.
  No run yet. The record's oil was 22 and the death sources were the titan and grease fiends, so this package would not have changed 18:27. The 6:07 and 3:21 wide lines named an oil exit and then another heading, and those ledgers were hot oil 140 and 109. The early oil wipe is still the open case.

- **Heart first** (untried): until Gravy Heart is 5/5, want is only Gravy Heart. Reroll anything else while a reroll remains; with no reroll take Butter Pat. Seeker Sprouts and Fertilizer wait. Tests whether the 111 cap is what lets one burst end the run.
  No run yet. Weaker as a full plan after 18:27, which had no heart and outlived the 183-bar run. Still the live counterfactual for the last hit: the bar fell from 99% to 16/111 while titan and grease fiends were the ledger. Delaying Overgrowth to stack the heart first is the part that disagrees with the record and with the Late Blight timing.

- **Heart before the second evolution** (untried): open Overgrowth on the sprout plan, then want only Gravy Heart until 5/5. Thousand Fries, Afterburner Fries, and Small Fry wait. Tests whether the 183 bar is the gap from 18:27 to 20:00.
  No run yet. The record went to weapons and Starch Plating instead and is the long run, so this is no longer the explanation of 15:22 versus 17:20. It remains the test of that one burst. The 18:27 run also survived Fryer King with oil at 22 and no heart.

- **Chives after the planned chests** (untried): Chives stays off want and off the no-reroll list until Thousand Fries and Hungry Sprouts are already evolved. Tests whether early Chives is what readies a tot weapon and holds the evolution chest.
  No run yet. The record had no Chives, Tot Toss still reached 8/8, and Thousand Fries still landed. The 17:20 run ended with Chives 5/5 and Tot Gatling. Early Chives is not required to explain either long build. Kept because the strategy still uses Chives as a no-reroll filler.

- **Wide build** (retired): fill all six weapon slots as early as possible, levelling whichever weapon is offered. Tests the weapons-held correlation in the run history directly.
  2026-11-07 Nightmare Daily, 6:07, level 17, 4302 kills. Six weapons full by the 6:03 telegraph, none evolved. Hot oil 140, sizzle-imps 73, Late Blight 11. Beat the old one- and two-weapon mean and is the floor of the six-weapon runs.
  2026-11-07 Nightmare Daily, second attempt, 3:21, level 9, 2012 kills. Three weapons, Seeker Sprouts 3/8, hot oil 109, Colorado Queen 2.
  Retired. The 18:27 run also ended on six weapons, with Overgrowth at 5:00 and Thousand Fries and Hungry Sprouts in the final inventory. Weapon count at the end is still a result. Filling slots before sprouts can reach 8 is the retired part.

- **Rats from 12:00** (retired): from The Mandoline on, every line is away from field rats and ice imps, and no chest is named even when the ground is clear. Tested whether the late ledger is a rat heading.
  No dedicated run. Settled by the serious 18:27 ledger: thinner-side walking from 12:00 through Deep Freezer took 26 from field rats and 0 listed ice imps, on a full bar through 17:30. The death sources were Compost Titan 144 and grease fiends 105 under a circle sidestep. A rat-only heading would not have named that fight. The 15:22 rat ledger (170) belonged to a line that stood on a circle clause from 14:00.
