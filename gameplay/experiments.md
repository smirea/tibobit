# Experiments

High-level strategies to try on experiment runs, so the serious strategy does not settle into a local maximum. The coach picks one per experiment run, plays it on purpose even where strategy.md disagrees, and rewrites this list after every run: new ideas go in, results and reasons go under each entry, and ideas that are settled get retired. Results of each experiment run are logged in experiments.csv.

## Ideas

- **Calm-bar chest** (untried): when a pair is ready, the boss is outside 80, nothing is underfoot, and the bar is not falling, open the chest even though the minute is still a boss minute. Tests the five-minute hold.
  No run yet. The 12:04 serious run had Big Fry ready by 7:02, a full bar with Sir Strawman 112 away and the Fryer King 181 away, and the chest still about 650 away at death. The 18:27 run kept boss-minute chests shut and lived longer, with Thousand Fries in by 16:28, so this still disagrees with the best run. A clean read is an open of Thousand Fries or Hungry Sprouts. Big Fry alone is the pair the 15:22 run already opened on its way to an earlier death.

- **Plating before the fourth weapon** (untried): after Overgrowth, Starch Plating to 5 before a fourth weapon is taken. Tests whether the 12:04 rat wipe is the missing plating row.
  No run yet. 12:04 ended with five weapons, Mash Finisher 3/5, Golden Fry, and no Starch Plating, and field rats dealt 290. 18:27 had Starch Plating 5/5 and Spry Sprout 5/5 and rats dealt 26, while also walking the thinner side with Thousand Fries and Hungry Sprouts. Plating alone is not identified. Disagrees with leveling the record weapons and the plating row together.

- **Two-boss edge** (untried): from Compost Titan's spawn, walk to the map edge away from the titan and keep moving along that edge once Ketchup Kraken spawns. Tests whether the thinner side still leaves you in the overlap that did titan 144 and grease fiends 105.
  No run yet. The 18:27 death was that overlap on a full 111 bar, with both bosses alive at 18:24 and the line a circle sidestep. The 12:04 serious run died at the Mandoline to field rats and never saw the titan. Disagrees with the thinner-side rule the strategy uses once the ground is clear.

- **Never heart** (untried): Gravy Heart is never in want. After Overgrowth, Starch Plating to 5, then Spry Sprout, and Salt Shaker is taken for Thousand Fries. Tests whether the record passive row repeats when it is the plan, and whether refusing the heart costs the 18:24 burst.
  No run yet. 18:27 landed that row by lockout: Spud Sneakers, Fertilizer 4/5, Sprouting Eyes, Starch Plating 5/5, Spry Sprout 5/5, Salt Shaker, and a 111 bar outlived the 17:20 heart build. 12:04 also had no heart, and also had no Starch Plating, and died at 12:04 to rats on a bar near 120. That is a different row. The last 18:27 drop was about 99% to 16/111, so a 183 bar is still the open counterfactual.

- **Thousand Fries first** (untried): after Overgrowth, want is only Fry Knives and Salt Shaker until that chest opens, then Hungry Sprouts. Other weapons wait. Tests whether Thousand Fries earlier than 16:28 beats spreading levels across Tailgate Fries, Frozen Fries, Locked Gatling, and Tot Toss.
  No run yet. 18:27 had both evolutions and those four weapons at 8, with Thousand Fries confirmed at 16:28. 12:04 did the opposite: Small Fry and Afterburner Fries were maxed, Fry Knives never appeared, and the run died at 12:04 with Big Fry shut. Disagrees with leveling the whole record set together.

- **Greedy** (untried): take every chest, shrine and gold pickup in reach and the highest-damage card every level. Tests whether the safety-first play is leaving power on the table.
  No run yet. 18:27 left chests closed through boss minutes and still finished Thousand Fries and Hungry Sprouts with every weapon maxed by 17:30. The death was a standstill in the titan overlap. 12:04 also held a ready chest shut, from 7:02 until death, and the ledger was field rats and hot oil. The held chest there was Big Fry, on a build with no Fry Knives. Closed chests remain open as a variable.

- **Safe** (untried): never walk toward anything worth having while any enemy is inside 100; take every health and armor card. Tests how far pure survival carries a weak build.
  No run yet. 18:27 was a standstill in the titan overlap, on a full bar, with bosses kept past 100. 12:04 died to rats on a weak passive row that already contained Mash Finisher and Golden Fry and still had an empty passive slot. Taking every health card can fill that slot with the same ban list. The early wide deaths were oil plus a second heading, which this package does not isolate.

- **Short-term focus** (untried): every pick is whatever kills the most enemies right now (new weapons first, then weapon levels), no recipe planning. Tests whether early kill speed beats evolution plans.
  No run yet. 18:27 reached level 74 and 81209 kills and died in the two-boss overlap. 15:22 reached 36822 kills and died on a 111 bar to rats. 12:04 reached level 45 and 25088 kills and died to field rats 290 with Overgrowth in and Big Fry unopened. Kill count past the early game keeps failing as an account of the long deaths.

- **Long-term focus** (untried): commit to two evolutions from the first level-up, reroll everything else, and open every chest the moment a pair is ready. Tests whether a strict plan reaches evolutions earlier than a flexible one.
  No run yet. 18:27 was the flexible version: Overgrowth at 5:00, Fertilizer at 4/5, heart missed, Thousand Fries and Hungry Sprouts late, plus the fries and gatling weapons. A strict reroll would have refused Salt Shaker, Starch Plating, and Spry Sprout. 12:04 was flexible in the other direction and spent the slots on Small Fry and Afterburner Fries. The strict plan is still untested.

- **XP vacuum** (untried): prioritize xp gems and pickup-radius cards (Potato Magnet) over everything but safety. Tests whether level count is the bottleneck.
  No run yet. 18:27 hit level 74 and two evolutions without hunting gems, and died on HP in the titan overlap. 17:20 ended at level 68 with the heart. 12:04 ended at level 45 because the run ended at 12:04, with field rats and hot oil as the ledger. Level count is not what separated 18:27 from a clear.

- **Evolve-then-wide** (untried): open Overgrowth before any extra weapon, then fill every remaining slot before Jet Fryer or Big Fry. Tests whether the extra weapons on the long runs extend a run only after the first evolution.
  No run yet. 18:27 had three extra weapons by 2:00, Overgrowth at 5:00, then the wide record set. 12:04 had Overgrowth by 5:00 and then filled Locked Gatling, Tot Toss, Small Fry, and Afterburner Fries, and died at 12:04 with Fry Knives still missing. The pure delay of every other weapon until Overgrowth is still open, and it has to allow Thousand Fries and the record weapons.

- **First ready pair** (untried): evolve whichever weapon first reaches 8 with its catalyst, and open that chest on the beat, even when the weapon is not Seeker Sprouts. Tests whether any evolution before Late Blight passes that minute.
  No run yet. 18:27's first evolution was Overgrowth at 5:00. At 10:03 Big Fry and Thousand Fries were both ready and the chest stayed closed for a circle; Thousand Fries was in by 16:28. 12:04 had Big Fry ready by 7:02 and kept it shut until death, so this experiment was not played. 15:22 did open Big Fry and Jet Fryer and reached 15:22. The deliberate on-the-beat open is still open.

- **Oil-exit minute** (untried): play the normal sprout plan. After the first hot-oil or puddle tick in a boss minute, every later line until the next minute names only stepping off that ground, with no thinner-side clause. Tests whether the wide deaths were the compound heading.
  No run yet. 18:27 took 22 oil and died to the titan and grease fiends. 6:07 and 3:21 named an oil exit and then another heading, and those ledgers were hot oil 140 and 109. 12:04 named the thinner side for the Fryer King minute and hot oil ended at 154. The strategy now keeps the exit as the whole line while ticks continue. This package still goes further: no thinner-side clause for the rest of that minute after the ground is clear.

- **Heart first** (untried): until Gravy Heart is 5/5, want is only Gravy Heart. Reroll anything else while a reroll remains; with no reroll take Butter Pat. Seeker Sprouts and Fertilizer wait. Tests whether the 111 cap is what lets one burst end the run.
  No run yet. 18:27 had no heart and outlived the 183-bar run. 12:04 wanted the heart for the whole run, never got a stack, and died at 12:04 on a bar near 120 with the plating row also missing. Delaying Overgrowth to stack the heart first still disagrees with the record and with the Late Blight timing.

- **Heart before the second evolution** (untried): open Overgrowth on the sprout plan, then want only Gravy Heart until 5/5. Thousand Fries, Afterburner Fries, and Small Fry wait. Tests whether the 183 bar is the gap from 18:27 to 20:00.
  No run yet. 18:27 went to weapons and Starch Plating and is the long run. 12:04 kept Gravy Heart in want beside Fry Knives and the side fryers, never saw the heart, and never opened the ready Big Fry chest. Heart-only until 5/5 remains untested. 18:27 also survived the Fryer King with oil at 22 and no heart.

- **Chives after the planned chests** (retired): Chives stays off want and off the no-reroll list until Thousand Fries and Hungry Sprouts are already evolved. Tests whether early Chives is what readies a tot weapon and holds the evolution chest.
  Settled without a dedicated run. 18:27 had no Chives, Tot Toss at 8/8, and Thousand Fries. 12:04 had no Chives and Tot Toss at 8/8 again. 17:20 had Chives 5/5 and Tot Gatling, which Chives was not required to explain. Early Chives does not ready the tot weapon. The serious no-reroll list can keep Chives as a late filler because the long heart build held it.

- **Wide build** (retired): fill all six weapon slots as early as possible, levelling whichever weapon is offered. Tests the weapons-held correlation in the run history directly.
  2026-11-07 Nightmare Daily, 6:07, level 17, 4302 kills. Six weapons full by the 6:03 telegraph, none evolved. Hot oil 140, sizzle-imps 73, Late Blight 11. Beat the old one- and two-weapon mean and is the floor of the six-weapon runs.
  2026-11-07 Nightmare Daily, second attempt, 3:21, level 9, 2012 kills. Three weapons, Seeker Sprouts 3/8, hot oil 109, Colorado Queen 2.
  Retired. 18:27 also ended on six weapons, with Overgrowth at 5:00 and Thousand Fries and Hungry Sprouts in the final inventory. 12:04 ended on five weapons, Overgrowth in, and still died at 12:04. Weapon count at the end is still a result. Filling slots before sprouts can reach 8 is the retired part.

- **Rats from 12:00** (retired): from The Mandoline on, every line is away from field rats and ice imps, and no chest is named even when the ground is clear. Tested whether the late ledger is a rat heading.
  No dedicated run. Settled by 18:27: thinner-side walking from 12:00 through Deep Freezer took 26 from field rats and 0 listed ice imps, on a full bar through 17:30. The death sources were Compost Titan 144 and grease fiends 105 under a circle sidestep. 15:22's rat ledger (170) belonged to a circle line that ran from 14:00. 12:04 then ran a rat-away line from 10:45 through the Mandoline and field rats dealt 290, with the boss absent from the ledger. A rat heading is the confirmed failure. The thinner side stays the serious rule.
