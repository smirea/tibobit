# Experiments

High-level strategies to try on experiment runs, so the serious strategy does not settle into a local maximum. The coach picks one per experiment run, plays it on purpose even where strategy.md disagrees, and rewrites this list after every run: new ideas go in, results and reasons go under each entry, and ideas that are settled get retired. Results of each experiment run are logged in experiments.csv.

## Ideas

- **Greedy** (untried): take every chest, shrine and gold pickup in reach and the highest-damage card every level. Tests whether the safety-first play is leaving power on the table.
  No run yet. Both wide runs left chests closed. The 6:07 run had Tailgate Fries at 8/8 from 3:04 with no catalyst, so that closed chest was a ready pair only if a catalyst had existed. The 3:21 run held nothing at 8 (Hot Potato 4/8, Frozen Fries 3/8, Seeker Sprouts 3/8) and died to hot oil 109, so a closed chest is not why it died. Taking pickups is still open.

- **Safe** (untried): never walk toward anything worth having while any enemy is inside 100; take every health and armor card. Tests how far pure survival carries a weak build.
  No run yet. The 3:21 wide run left chests, shrines, and food alone while a boss was up and still died with hot oil 109 and the queen 2. That line also appended a thinner-side heading, and the level-ups did not take health cards, so the Safe package is still open.

- **Short-term focus** (untried): every pick is whatever kills the most enemies right now (new weapons first, then weapon levels), no recipe planning. Tests whether early kill speed beats evolution plans.
  No run yet. The 6:07 wide run leveled whatever weapon was offered and the 6:00 screen still dealt hot oil 140 and sizzle-imps 73, but the quota was empty slots, not kill speed. The 3:21 run leveled weapons already in want, finished at 2012 kills and level 9, and its ledger was oil. Kill speed as the deliberate rule is still open.

- **Long-term focus** (untried): commit to two evolutions from the first level-up, reroll everything else, and open every chest the moment a pair is ready. Tests whether a strict plan reaches evolutions earlier than a flexible one.
  No run yet. The two no-plan wide runs died at 3:21 and 6:07 with no evolution, which is the opposite of this commit, so the strict plan is still open.

- **Wide build** (retired): fill all six weapon slots as early as possible, levelling whichever weapon is offered. Tests the weapons-held correlation in the run history directly.
  2026-11-07 Nightmare Daily, 6:07, level 17, 4302 kills. Six weapons were full by the 6:03 telegraph, none evolved: Seeker Sprouts 3/8, Tailgate Fries 8/8 (already 8/8 at 3:04), Tot Toss 3/8, Reset Button 1/8, Small Fry 2/8, Afterburner Fries 2/8. Passives Spud Sneakers 2/5, Sprouting Eyes 1/5, Butter Pat 1/5. Two slots were still open at 2:00, 2:30, and 3:04; one slot was still open at 4:02 and 5:00; the sixth landed between 5:00 and 6:03. Health 61/111 at 2:30, 91/111 at the queen telegraph, full at The Masher and at the 6:03 telegraph, dead at 6:07. Damage: hot oil 140, sizzle-imps 73, hay-bales 19, Late Blight 11, Amusing 3, fry-flies 2, Colorado Queen 2, crumb-ant 1. Beat the 4:32 one- and two-weapon mean, lost to the 6:38 best short run, and is the floor of the six-weapon runs. A full bar of unevolved weapons still died in the Late Blight minute, and Late Blight's own 11 damage leaves oil and sizzle-imps as the sources big enough to remove that bar.
  2026-11-07 Nightmare Daily, second attempt, 3:21, level 9, 2012 kills. The slot plan stalled at three weapons: Seeker Sprouts 3/8, Hot Potato 4/8, Frozen Fries 3/8. Passives Mash Finisher 1/5, GPU 1/5, Bacon Bits 1/5, taken on weaponless screens with no reroll. At 2:05 Frozen Fries leveled and Small Fry was skipped; Small Fry was not in the standing want. Alive at 2:30 with three weapon slots still empty, dead at 3:21. Damage: hot oil 109, Colorado Queen 2. Under the 4:32 one- and two-weapon mean. The six-slot condition never arrived, and the queen chip stayed at 2 while the compound oil-then-thinner line was the advice from 0:30 on.
  Retired, and the second attempt stays retired: early weapons without an evolution died at 6:07 once the slots were full and at 3:21 when they were not. Weapon count at the end is not a plan for the first minutes.

- **XP vacuum** (untried): prioritize xp gems and pickup-radius cards (Potato Magnet) over everything but safety. Tests whether level count is the bottleneck.
  No run yet. The 6:07 wide run ignored pickups and finished level 17, the same level as the 6:37 run. The 3:21 wide run finished level 9, the same level as the 3:16 one-weapon death, so this clock does not show an XP shortage relative to other deaths at that time. Pickup priority is still open.

- **Evolve-then-wide** (untried): open Overgrowth before any extra weapon, then fill every remaining slot before starting Jet Fryer or Big Fry. The wide runs are the missing half: six slots and no evolution died at 6:07, and three slots and no evolution died at 3:21.

  No run yet. The 3:21 run died with Hot Potato 4/8 and Frozen Fries 3/8, so no pair was ready and the chest rule was not tested.

Tests whether the wide deaths were the compound heading. Both wide lines named an oil exit and then another heading; the ledgers were hot oil 140 at 6:07 and hot oil 109 at 3:21, with the queen at 2 each time.
  No run yet.

- **Heart first** (untried): until Gravy Heart is 5/5, want is only Gravy Heart. Reroll anything else while a reroll remains; with no reroll take Butter Pat. Seeker Sprouts and Fertilizer wait until the bar is stacked. Tests whether a 111 max HP is what lets one oil soak end the run. Gravy Heart 5/5 raised the 17:20 bar to 183. The 3:21 heartless ledger was hot oil 109 and the queen 2. Disagrees with Fertilizer once and Seeker Sprouts to 8 first.
  No run yet. A single Gravy Heart stack already failed to save the 3:36 fly death (heart 1/5, sprouts 5/8), so the test is the stacked bar.
