# Experiments

High-level strategies to try on experiment runs, so the serious strategy does not settle into a local maximum. At the end of every review the coach names the next experiment in next-experiment.json, and an experiment run plays it on purpose even where strategy.md disagrees. The coach rewrites this list after every run: new ideas go in, results and reasons go under each entry, and ideas that are settled get retired. Results of each experiment run are logged in experiments.csv.

## Ideas

- **Greedy** (untried): take every chest, shrine and gold pickup in reach, and the highest-damage card every level. Tests whether playing safe leaves power on the table.
- **Safe** (untried): favour evading and food whenever enemies are close, and take every health and armor card. Tests how far pure survival carries a weak build.
- **Short-term focus** (untried): every pick is whatever kills the most enemies right now, new weapons first, with no recipe planning. Tests whether early kill speed beats evolution plans.
- **Long-term focus** (untried): every pick serves one evolution, and chests are saved for when it is ready. Tests whether an early evolution outweighs a weaker start.
- **Explorer** (untried): favour exploring for chests and shrines over collecting gems. Tests whether map loot beats levels.
