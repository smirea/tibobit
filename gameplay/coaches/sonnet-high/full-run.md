# Full run

This is iteration 10: the bot plays one whole game live with your playbook, on the evaluation seed, in a freshly loaded tab. It is not a test. Put the best you know into practice.

## The goal

Survive 20:00. A win moves your Nightmare ladder up one level, and the campaign ends at a win on Nightmare 10. Time after 20:00 does not matter.

## Choosing the playbook

Use what your beliefs and live experiments support, not what you hope works. Ship a change from the baseline only if paired live games on at least 5 seeds agree (for example 4 of 5 alive at 5:00 against the 1 of 6 baseline). Lockstep results are leads, not support. If no live evidence exists for a change, keep the baseline but read its goal text for rules that fail near a boss or when HP is mid-range, since the L3 run died while the text ranked Explore above Evade with the Mascot 45 units away. The baseline is steam-vent, the chest-first goal text with the 10:00 gold rule, default picks and the current knobs; it won Nightmare 1 under lockstep and lives roughly 1 of 6 live on NM2.

## Generalizing

The evaluation seed is fixed so that full runs can be compared. Do not tune anything to its map or to what happened on it before: the playbook has to win on any seed.
