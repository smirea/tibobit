# Full run

This is iteration 10: the bot plays one whole game with your playbook, on the evaluation seed, in a freshly loaded tab. It is not a test. Put the best you know into practice.

## The goal

Survive 20:00. A win moves your Nightmare ladder up one level, and the campaign ends at a win on Nightmare 10. Time after 20:00 does not matter.

## Choosing the playbook

Use what your beliefs and experiments support, not what you hope works. If two choices are equally supported (two agreeing games each), keep the one that has already played well. The baseline, steam-vent, lockstep 1 and the chest-first goal text with the 10:00 gold rule, won Nightmare 1 (24:47). Changes from it need agreeing games at the level being played (check the ladder level first), especially for the opening 0-10:00 and the 14:00-20:00 bosses.

## Generalizing

The evaluation seed is fixed so that full runs can be compared. Do not tune anything to its map or to what happened on it before: the playbook has to win on any seed.
