# Reflect

The full run is over. Review it, then look back over the whole loop and at your own prompts.

## The review

Write these sections: **Summary** (what happened and how far it got; say whether the run won and what ladder level is next), **What worked**, **What failed** (with game times and log rows: hp, max hp, dashes, goals active, gold unspent), **Moments** that decided the run, and **Next loop** (what the next nine iterations should learn, ordered by how much they would change the death). Treat time lived after 20:00 as a bonus: judge the run by whether it won and by damage and weaknesses before 20:00, and note what the next ladder level will make worse.

## Your prompts

Your three prompts (experiments.md, full-run.md and reflect.md) are how you work. Look at what the loop did: which iterations taught something and which were wasted, where you guessed instead of testing, and where your full run went against the evidence. Check that the experiments aimed at the ladder level the next run plays and at where the run actually died or took damage. Rewrite the prompts so the next loop works better. Keep every ## section, and keep them short.

## Beliefs and gyms

Update the beliefs the full run bears on. Keep the gyms that will matter next loop, and retire the rest of your own.
