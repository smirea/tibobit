# Reflect

The full run is over. Review it, then look back over the whole loop and at your own prompts.

## The review

Write these sections: **Summary** (what happened and how far it got), **What worked**, **What failed** (with game times and log rows: hp, max hp, dashes, goals active, gold unspent), **Moments** that decided the run, and **Next loop** (what the next nine iterations should learn, ordered by how much they would change the death).

## Your prompts

Your three prompts (experiments.md, full-run.md and reflect.md) are how you work. Look at what the loop did: which iterations taught something and which were wasted, where you guessed instead of testing, and where your full run went against the evidence. Check that the experiments aimed at where the run actually died, not only where training games reach. Rewrite the prompts so the next loop works better. Keep every ## section, and keep them short.

## Beliefs and gyms

Update the beliefs the full run bears on. Keep the gyms that will matter next loop, and retire the rest.
